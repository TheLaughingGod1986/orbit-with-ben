# YouTube growth and policy

Locked 25 Sep 2026. Cursor rule: `.cursor/rules/orbit-studio.mdc`.

The algorithm follows the audience.

## Get more views

- Picture already moving: a long in the first 3 seconds, a Short in the first second. No slow intro card.
- The thumbnail grabs attention. The title creates curiosity. They are not the same words, and both match the film.
- A live film under about 4% CTR needs a new thumb or title. Stay in the 4–7% band without breaking the promise.
- Cut pauses, frozen stills, and fluff. Average percentage viewed above about 50% is the hold to aim for. YouTube promotes a film that keeps people watching.

## Keep them watching

- First 30 seconds: start on the promised picture. Sentence one is that promise. No logo, no theme song, no introduction of who we are. Hint at the later payoff.
- About every 4–6 seconds, change the picture, put the named thing on screen, or add a quiet sound cue when a graphic appears. A Short stays 22–27 seconds.
- Move the story with cause and effect. A flat list of facts loses people.
- No goodbye. Do not say “in conclusion,” “to wrap up,” “thanks for watching,” or “that’s all.” Finish the last point and use the Studio end screen for the next film.

## Stay in good standing

- No music, movie clips, or game footage we do not have the rights to.
- Titles and tags match the film.
- No heavy swearing in the first 30 seconds, no extreme violence, no controversial medical advice.

## Consistency

One long a week and three Shorts. A finished film beats three rushed ones.
