## Primary strategy

**Every long-form video is the pillar.**

Every Short exists to **support, promote, or expand** that pillar.

The long-form video is **always** the priority for:

- Watch time
- Subscriber conversion
- Suggested / browse authority
- Monetisation readiness

Shorts are a discovery engine. They are never the product.

```
Pillar documentary (Day 1)
        ↓
Premium mini-doc Shorts (Day 1 evening → Day 7)
        ↓
Shorts feed discovery
        ↓
Related / soft CTA → pillar long-form
        ↓
Watch time · subs · returning viewers
        ↓
Suggested for next Wednesday pillar
```

## Publishing order (every release)

### Day 1 — Pillar first

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Publish the **long-form** first |
| 2 | Window: **18:00–20:00 UK** (ops lock: **19:00**) |
| 3 | Title, description, chapters, thumbnail, end screens, cards — **final before go-live** |

### Day 1 — First Short (1–3 hours later)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 4 | Publish Short #1 from the **strongest hook** or most intriguing moment |
| 5 | Ops lock: **21:00 UK** (≈ 2 hours after the long) |
| 6 | Related video → this week’s long-form (desktop Studio; primary Short → long CTA). Pin optional if Related is set.

### Weekly rhythm — Wednesday long, then related Shorts

| Day | What | Time UK |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Wednesday** | Long-form | **19:00** (cold-start exception allowed) |
| **Thu–Tue** | Related Shorts (**3–5** discovery; extras as reserves) | **12:30** (optional Day-1 launch Short ~21:00) |
| **Next Wednesday** | Next long-form | **19:00** |

Example: long on Wednesday → Shorts Thursday through Tuesday about that long → next long the following Wednesday.

**Growth System v2 spacing**

- Target **3–5** discovery Shorts per long (curiosity-gap ends + Related → pillar); hold soft-duplicates as **Private** reserves.
- Shorts must promote **that week’s** long only.
- One Short per day max — never dump the cluster on Day 1.
- Unused soft-duplicates stay **Private** — do not park them in December.
- Current backlog ships through **August–September**.

**Never** publish a teaser Short *before* the pillar is public.  
**Never** dump the whole cluster on Day 1.  
**Never** fill a gap with a Short from another pillar.

## Shorts strategy

### Do not cut random clips

Each Short must work as a **complete standalone mini-documentary**.

### Every Short must

1. Hook within the **first 2 seconds** with the **strongest fact** (on-screen + VO)
2. Focus on **one** fascinating fact, mystery, or question
3. Deliver value **independently** of the long
4. End with an **unanswered question / curiosity gap**
5. Link via YouTube **Related video** (desktop Studio) → that Short’s Thursday long — never only “Watch the full video.” Never another Short. Never a dead id. Pin not required if Related is set. See `.cursor/rules/orbit-shorts-related-video.mdc`.

### On-screen text (TikTok / Shorts lock)

Mirror the space-doc kinetic style that retains (yellow/white bold lowercase, 1–3 words/beat). Spec: `TikTok/SHORTS_ONSCREEN_TEXT_STYLE.md`. Builders: `*_shorts_v02.py`.

Funnel + cross-post lock: `SHORTS_FUNNEL_AND_CROSSPOST.md` — Related video required (desktop Studio), pin optional, monster-hook first in the cluster, YouTube at T0 then TikTok at **T0 + 1 hour** (TikTok uploads paused until ban lift).

### Arc (canonical: **22–30s** micro-stories)

See `docs/ORBIT_GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md`. Opening cliff is P0 (largest drop 0–8s).
Default **22–30s**. Do not stretch a 20s idea to 45–60s.

```
0–1.5s  Hook — strongest fact; speech starts immediately
1.5–6s  Curiosity escalation (do not fully answer)
6–18s   Core evidence
18–27s  Partial payoff / twist
last 1–3s  Curiosity gap → Related long (not bare “watch the full video”)
```

Every Short is a **standalone micro-story**. Longer cuts only when retention data earns them.

### Curiosity-gap endings (preferred)

- “There is one reason scientists fear the Great Filter. Orbit discovers it in the full documentary.”
- “What Orbit finds past this point changes the whole story.”
- “The full answer is stranger than this.”
- “This is only one part of a much bigger mystery — Related points to the full film.”

Avoid: bare “WATCH NOW” / “Watch the full video.” · “LINK IN BIO” energy · hard-sell captions · fake urgency.

### Quality bar

Every Short should feel like a **premium mini-documentary**:

- Burned-in captions
- Vertical 1080×1920
- Orbit present as guide, not decoration
- Same science voice as the long
- No conspiracy framing

- [ ] Short #1–#N VO generated
- [ ] Long scheduled private → public Thu 19:00
- [ ] Shorts uploaded private / scheduled

### Release week

Follow `RELEASE_WEEK_CHECKLIST.md`.

### Next-week overlap

While Days 5–7 Shorts of week N publish, week N+1 long is already in VO / edit. Never start next week’s long on Thursday morning.

## Metadata principles

Always prioritise:

- Viewer retention
- Curiosity
- Storytelling
- Educational value
- Consistency
- High-quality thumbnails
- Strong titles
- Search-friendly metadata
- Evergreen topics

**Titles:** Question + Mystery + Emotion  
**Thumbs:** one idea · ≤ ~4 words on-image · Orbit only if he raises curiosity  
**Descriptions:** hook paragraph → summary → chapters → soft CTA → sources

## Analytics (Monday 30 min)

Score last pillar + its Shorts cluster:

| Metric | Healthy |
|--------|---------|
| Long CTR | 5–10% |
| Long AVD | 40%+ |
| Short → long traffic | Rising after Day 1 Short |
| Subs / long view | Climbing week over week |

**Kill rules**

- Thumb CTR &lt; 3% after ~1k impressions → replace
- Long AVD &lt; 30% → reopen edit (do not lengthen)
- Short swipe-away high vs peers → retire that hook style

- Long-form first. Always.
- Shorts are mini-docs, not ads or random cuts.
- Soft curiosity CTAs only.
- Science over speculation; wonder over certainty.
- Family-friendly; no conspiracy bait.
- One idea per Short; one story spine per long.
