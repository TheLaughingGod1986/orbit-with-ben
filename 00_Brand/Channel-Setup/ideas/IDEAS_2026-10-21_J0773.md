Smith** (`1931–2018`) and the **Voyager 2** team imaged Hyperion in *Science* on **January 29, 1982** (`PMID 17771273`), MIT planetary scientist **Jack Wisdom** (`born 1953`) and colleagues showed that Hyperion's irregular shape, `0.1042` eccentric orbit, and `3:4` Titan resonance prevent tidal locking: it is the **only regular planetary moon in the Solar System known not to be tidally locked**, rotating roughly every **13 days** while tumbling chaotically with a **Lyapunov time of around 30 days**, so its spin orientation is impossible to predict (`PIA20512`).
