### Notes on each

1. **Apophis 2029.** The familiar thing is our own sky. In 2004 it was the most dangerous asteroid ever found (Torino 4, about 1 in 37 for 2029); now we know it misses, and on Friday 13 April 2029 a ~340 m rock passes inside the ring of geostationary satellites, bright enough to see by eye from the UK. Danger turns to wonder in the story itself. Dated (2004, 2021, 2029), with people (Tucker, Tholen, Bernardi), a scale anchor (the Eiffel Tower) and a live news hook (OSIRIS-APEX). Subject words: *Apophis 2029, asteroid close approach, asteroid flyby, planetary defense*.
2. **Magnetic field flip.** The familiar thing is the compass and the shield over our heads. Strong dates (Brunhes–Matuyama about 780,000 years ago, Laschamp about 41,000 years ago) and people (Bernard Brunhes, Motonori Matuyama). Risk: easy to slide into dread framing ("we're overdue"); must end on wonder.
