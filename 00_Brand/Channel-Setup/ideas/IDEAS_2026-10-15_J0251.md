### Notes on each

1. **What Happens When Earth's Magnetic Field Flips?** (Runner-up in J0179, now #1 beyond 030 Apophis.) The familiar thing is the compass in your hand and the invisible shield overhead. Magnetic north has sprinted from Canada toward Siberia, the global field has weakened by about 9% since measurements began in the 1830s, and over South America a weak spot (the South Atlantic Anomaly) already forces the Hubble Space Telescope to pause observations as it passes through. Yet the fossil and ice records show that when the field flips — as it last did 780,000 years ago (Brunhes–Matuyama), and briefly tried to 41,000 years ago (Laschamp) — it does not vanish overnight and does not strip away our air. Earth's thick atmosphere shields the ground, while multiple magnetic poles paint curtains of aurora across mid-latitude and tropical skies. Superb historical people (Bernard Brunhes 1906, Motonori Matuyama 1929), concrete numbers in every chapter, zero overlap with 001–030. Subject words: *Earth magnetic field flip, geomagnetic reversal, magnetic poles flipping, South Atlantic Anomaly, Laschamp event*.
2. **What Would Happen If You Landed on Titan?** Directly backed by scheduled standalone Short `RLSJm59OSzI` (*Standing on Titan Feels Like Diving 15 Metres Down*, airing 10 Nov 2026). Every familiar earthly rule is inverted: orange nitrogen smog 50% denser than Earth's air, −179 °C cold where water is bedrock and methane falls as slow-motion rain, Huygens's 72-minute transmission from the surface in 2005, and NASA's nuclear rotorcraft *Dragonfly* (launching 2028). Slight adjacency to 021 Saturn's Rings, though Titan is a surface-survival and alien-weather story rather than ring physics.
3. **The Strangest Things We Left in Space (backlog 019 / `015_Left-In-Space`).** Backed by vidIQ keyword volume (`voyager golden record` ~12.6k/mo, score 62.9) and scheduled standalone Short `kKN8Ale42sE` (*Voyager 1 Is About to Be One Light-Day Away*, airing 17 Nov 2026). 4. **Why This Planet Has 21-Year Winters (Uranus).** Backed by scheduled standalone Short `ZpEA8stoSPA` (airing 3 Nov 2026).
