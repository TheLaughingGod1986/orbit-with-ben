### 1. `055` — *What Will Humans Become in 1,000 Years? What Spaceflight Does to the Human Body* (SELECTED FOR `055`)
- **Backlog ID:** `008` (`VIDEO_BACKLOG.json`, *Future Humanity* pillar).
- **Why #1 now:**
  1. **Direct continuity lock:** `054` (*Are We Living Inside a Simulation? What Physics Actually Says*) explicitly promises *"what spaceflight does to the human body"* in its subscribe beat (Line 40) and *"next, we step inside the real biological stakes of space exploration to ask what spaceflight does to the human body, and what humans will become in 1,000 years"* in its closing sentence (Line 80).
  2. **Highest unscaffolded score in the 20 Oct weekly audit (`J0693`):** Ranked #1 in `05_Analytics/weekly_audit/OWB_2026-10-20.md` Section 5, backed by Astrum's **1.97M-view** *The Surprising Danger of Building a Human Colony on the Moon* (`UPWQimElMhQ`), Kurzgesagt's human-body science hits, and OWB's second-person physical survival hook (`What Happens If You Fall Into a Black Hole?` at `50.4%` AVD; `Why You Can't Stand On Jupiter` at `4.9%` CTR).
  3. **Concrete quantitative biology & physics landmarks (no sci-fi hand-waving):**
     - **The NASA Twins Study (March 2015 – March 2016; Garrett-Bakelman et al., *Science* April 2019, `PMID 30975860` & `PMID 32375154`, NASA Twins Study releases):** Identical twin astronauts **Scott Kelly** (`born 1964`, `340 days` aboard the ISS) and **Mark Kelly** (`born 1964`, ground control on Earth), studied by **10 research teams**: telomere elongation during flight followed by rapid shortening upon landing, **about 91.3%** of gene-expression shifts reverting to baseline while a persistent subset remained altered after **6 months**, **7% body mass decrease**, carotid artery wall thickening, and elevated **AQP2** water-reabsorption protein linked to cephalad fluid shifts and Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome (**SANS**).
     - **Microgravity Bone Loss & High-Resolution Trabecular CT (Neginhal & George, `PMID 42729855`; Leigh Gabel et al. 2022, *Scientific Reports*, `PMID 35773442`):** Weight-bearing bone mineral density falls **1% to 2% per month** in microgravity; 3D high-resolution peripheral quantitative computed tomography (**HR-pQCT** at **61 micrometers**) of **17 astronauts** (mean age **47 years**) showed **9 of 17 astronauts** did not fully recover tibial bone mineral density **12 months** post-flight, with missions longer than **6 months** leaving a **-3.9%** median deficit in bone failure load (vs **-0.4%** for missions under 6 months) — equal to **a decade or more** of terrestrial aging.
     - **Interplanetary Cosmic Radiation in Transit to Mars (Cary Zeitlin, Donald Hassler et al., *Science* May 2013, `PMID 23723233` & `PMID 27662785`):** Launched **November 26, 2011**, NASA's Mars Science Laboratory (*Curiosity*) measured galactic cosmic rays with its Radiation Assessment Detector (**RAD**) across its **253-day**, **560-million-kilometer** cruise to Mars, finding a round-trip transit dose equivalent of **0.66 ± 0.12 sieverts** (`660 millisieverts`) even for the shortest round-trip with current propulsion and shielding.
     - **Living on Mars at `0.38 g` and Surface Radiation (Donald Hassler et al., *Science* 2014, `PMID 24324275` & `PMID 31421853`; NASA Mars Fact Sheet):** Starting **August 7, 2012**, Curiosity's RAD measured Mars's surface radiation over **~300 days** beneath a mean surface gravity of **3.73 m/s²** (**0.380** of Earth's **9.82 m/s²**), while **Hermann Joseph Muller** (`1890–1967`) proved in **1927** that ionizing radiation directly accelerates genetic mutation rates.
     - **Human Evolutionary Speed Over 1,000 Years (Charles Darwin, `1809–1882`; Xin Yi & Rasmus Nielsen et al., *Science* July 2010, `PMID 20595611`):** Sequencing **50 Tibetan exomes** (`92%` of human genes at **18x** coverage) revealed a **78% allele frequency difference** at the hypoxia transcription factor gene **EPAS1** between Tibetan and Han populations — the fastest allele frequency change ever observed in a human gene — proving that strong environmental selection across **33 to 40 generations** (**1,000 years**) alongside **0.38 g** gravity and subsurface radiation shielding can measurably remodel human physiology and genetics.
