## 1. Publishing order (non-negotiable)

1. **Publish the long-form first** (Day 1, Thu **19:00** UK — inside 18:00–20:00).
2. **1–3 hours later** → Short #1 (ops: **21:00**) from the strongest hook.
3. **Days 2–7** → one Short per day @ **12:30** UK.

Never a teaser Short before the pillar is public.  
Never dump the cluster on Day 1.

---

## 2. Best publishing days & times

### Long-form → **Thursday 19:00 UK**

| Factor | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Algorithm | Mid-week upload → Fri–Sun watch accumulation |
| Audience | Sit-down science/doc evenings |
| Ops | Mon–Wed runway; Shorts cut from locked master |
| Window | Matches 18:00–20:00 UK brief |

### Shorts

| When | Time | Role |
|------|------|------|
| Thu (Day 1) | **21:00** | Strongest hook · halo while long is fresh |
| Fri–Wed | **12:30** | Midday discovery · one idea each |

### Spacing

- Longs: **7 days** apart
- Shorts: **one per day** after Day 1 Short
- Cluster: **5–7** Shorts / long (default **6**)

## 4. Content flywheel

```
Long film (Thu 19:00)
        ↓
5–7 premium mini-doc Shorts (Thu 21:00 → Wed)
        ↓
Shorts discovery
        ↓
Related / soft CTA → long film
        ↓
Watch time + subs
        ↓
Social + Community pack
        ↓
Next Thursday pillar
```

Every long also generates: 3 X · 3 Threads · 2 LinkedIn · 3 Facebook · 1 Reddit · 1 Community poll · 1 Community image · (future email + blog).

See `CONTENT_FLYWHEEL_TEMPLATE.md` + `RELEASE_WEEK_CHECKLIST.md`.

### Short packaging rules

- Hook in **≤2 seconds**
- One fascinating fact / mystery / question
- Standalone value
- End with curiosity (soft funnel — never an ad)
- Related video → **this** Thursday long once public (desktop Studio; required). Pin optional if Related is set.

## 8. Analytics review

### Monday scorecard

1. Last long: CTR, AVD, retained @30s/50%/90%, subs, traffic  
2. Each Short: 48h views, viewed %, swipe-away, traffic to long  
3. One decision only  
4. Update backlog status  

### Kill rules

- Thumb CTR &lt; 3% after ~1k impressions → replace  
- AVD &lt; 30% → reopen edit  
- Weak Short hook style → retire pattern
