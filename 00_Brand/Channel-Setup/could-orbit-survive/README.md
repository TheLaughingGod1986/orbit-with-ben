# Could Orbit Survive…? (test format)

Started 24 Sep 2026. It runs in the Wednesday Short slot for three test weeks. See the four-week test plan in [FAMILIAR_DANGER_STRATEGY.md](../FAMILIAR_DANGER_STRATEGY.md#four-week-test-plan-12-oct--6-nov).

## Why

The Shorts that won on this channel promise something happening to a body: *Why You Can't Stand on a Neutron Star* (419), *What Remains After the Last Star Dies?* (622). So far that body has been "you", with Orbit off to the side. In this format **Orbit is the body.** Orbit goes to the dangerous place and the place acts on Orbit. No other space channel has that, and a character people come back for is what turns viewers into subscribers.

## The shape (22–27 seconds)

| Time | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | The place, already moving. The danger in action. Hook caption, 3–5 words. **No Orbit before about 1 s.** | Line 1, 5–7 words: what the place does. |
| ~1 s | Orbit arrives in the danger: falling, frosting, being pulled. Something visibly happens to Orbit. | Line 2, the stay line: "Stay, and I'll show you…" |
| 3–18 s | The place acting on Orbit, one real number on screen. The promoted film's title on screen at 9–14 s. | Two or three hard facts, one of them a real probe or measurement. |
| 18–25 s | Orbit gets out in its own way (climbs, dives, pulls away). Wonder, not harm: Orbit is never broken or melted on screen. | The payoff line. |
| last 4 s | The opening picture again, so the Short loops. | Loops into line 1. |

## Rules

- **Public titles say "a robot", not "Orbit".** A stranger in the feed doesn't know who Orbit is. Orbit is named in the captions once, after the hook.
- One Orbit, one face, cream eyes with pupils, no legs, one bottom glow. It follows the canonical still (`CHANNEL_AUTHORITY.md`).
- Every number is a real measurement or mission, and the source is noted in the script.
- The thumbnail follows house rules: the picture, yellow hook word, white rest, no Orbit.
- Studio Related points at the long the script names.
- Every export passes `gate_shorts_open.py check` before upload.

## How it's judged

Compare each Orbit Short with the Monday and Friday Shorts **in the same week**, which share the same channel state. Record stayed-to-watch at 48 hours, whether the retention graph ends above 100%, and subscribers gained.

- If the Orbit Short beats both same-week Shorts in two of the three weeks, it becomes a standing weekly series.
- If it loses all three weeks, stop the format.
