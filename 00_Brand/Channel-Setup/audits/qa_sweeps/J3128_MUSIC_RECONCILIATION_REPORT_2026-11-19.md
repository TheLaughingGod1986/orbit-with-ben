## 3. Deduplicated Comparison Library & Explicit Exclusion Rules

To ensure no earlier music beds were silently omitted when films moved from the local git checkouts to `/Volumes/data/studio-live` or `/Volumes/data/mac-mini-archive`, all 232 `05_Music` audio files across all three storage tiers were hashed and deduplicated by `(channel, sha256)` into **115 unique canonical music beds** (**31 OWB** + **84 HOS**):

1. **Same-bed self-exclusion (`identical_bed_sha256_self`):** A target bed is not compared against byte-identical copies of itself in the same film's `05_Music` folder across checkout/NAS/archive tiers, except where a cut intentionally reuses a cross-folder bed covered by an existing `music_gate_*_chief_waiver.json` (`owb_dated_2026_11_24_supermoon_v04`, `owb_ar2_iss_2030_deorbit_v14`, `J01_Journey_v03`, `owb_dated_2026_11_30_moon_jupiter_v01`), where the 1.0 match is recorded and waived via the pre-existing Chief waiver.
2. **Same-film `05_Music` exclusion (`same_film_05_music`):** Following `music_gate.py`'s `others_default()` (`film_dir.resolve() not in p.resolve().parents`), beds that reside strictly inside the same film's own `05_Music` folder (e.g., earlier draft versions or sister Short beds of that same film) are excluded and listed in each report's `excluded_same_film_or_self` array.
3. **Downstream compilation linking stem exclusion (`OWB:026` vs `j01_journey_linking_bed_v01.wav`):** In `OWB:J01` (`036_J01_From-Earth-To-The-Edge-Of-The-Universe`), `j01_journey_linking_bed_v01.wav` is an extracted 180 s linking slice taken directly from `OWB:026`'s owned `nearest-star_score_bed_v01_full.wav` (documented in `j01_journey_linking_bed_v01_plan.json`: *"reused from the owned 026 nearest-star stem for the Journey linking sections"*). When evaluating `OWB:026`'s own cuts (`026_NearestStar_v02` and its 3 Shorts), `j01_journey_linking_bed_v01.wav` is recorded as a downstream compilation derivative of `OWB:026`'s own bed rather than an earlier film's bed.
4. **Cross-channel UAT inclusion (`OWB UAT/jupiter-music.mp3` -> `OWB:013` `moon-leaving-part03_score_bed_v01.mp3`):** Because all 29 HOS entries originally attempted to compare against `OWB UAT/jupiter-music.mp3`, its byte-identical NAS equivalent (`moon-leaving-part03_score_bed_v01.mp3`, SHA-256 `38ab6e3e876860e72c49c547df6cbe1d05a82e021e6d517e4e64478db6f8de2b`) is explicitly included in all 48 OWB and all 29 HOS reconciled comparisons.
