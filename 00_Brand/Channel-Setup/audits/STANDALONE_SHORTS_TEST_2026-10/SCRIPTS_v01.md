## How these are built

- **Length:** 18–22 s. At the narrator's measured pace (56 words ≈ 23 s on *Could a Robot Survive on Europa?*) that's **44–52 spoken words**. The counts below are of the `>` lines.
- **Shape:** line 1 is the claim, with the titled subject moving at frame 0 and a whoosh/impact SFX. Line 2 is the stay line, and Orbit arrives about 1 s in, caught up in the moment, never at frame 0. One hard fact follows, then the payoff. The last line runs back into line 1 and the last ~3 s return to the opening picture so it loops.
- **New topics:** none of the 8 subjects has an Orbit long (001–029) or a past Short. Checked against every title in the 12 Oct snapshot.
- **On screen:** the hook caption on frame 0 (2–4 words, yellow hook word). At 9–14 s comes the **Short's own title**, because there's no long to name. At most one *Teach on screen* overlay, never in 0–2 s or over the 9–14 s title, inside the 16:9 band at 11–12% band height.
- **Spoken end:** no "watch the full film". These follow the week-2 rule (end on the payoff, last line runs into line 1).
- **Slots:** Tuesdays and Saturdays at 11:30 UK, 20 Oct – 14 Nov. The house Shorts keep Mon/Wed/Fri and the longs keep Sunday. The 12 Nov length-test Short (Thu) is clear. The scheduled Short dates checked on 12 Oct are 12, 16, 19, 21 and 23 Oct and 9, 11, 12, 13 and 16 Nov, none of them a Tue or Sat, so it's never two Shorts in a day.
