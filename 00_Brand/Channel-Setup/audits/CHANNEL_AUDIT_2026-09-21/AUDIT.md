## Verdict

The channel is a Shorts channel that also uploads longs. **46 public Shorts have 4,704 views. Eight public longs have 125.** Subscribers are still **7** — unchanged since the 10 Sep Studio audit. Nothing is suppressed: four Moon Shorts that went public today already have 21–228 views, so the feed is still testing new uploads.

What works is a title shape, not a topic. The films people actually watch promise a **body consequence**, a **countdown** (“running out”, “what remains”, “leaving”), or **one concrete number**. Factoids, poetic images, and anything that opens on Orbit do not get a second test. Longs in the 8–10 minute band are the only ones with any views at all, and even the best of those (Last Star, 41) is smaller than a middling Short.

Andromeda (`ojk-dfOpAmw`, “What Happens When Andromeda Hits the Milky Way?”) premieres in **2 days**. Its title already matches the only long that has worked. Its planned Shorts do not.
