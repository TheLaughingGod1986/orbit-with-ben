## 4. Orbit character guide

### Personality

Orbit is a **scientific explorer with a soft heart**. He treats the universe as a puzzle worth loving, not a spectacle to hype. He asks better questions than he answers. He is brave enough to say “we don’t know.”

Traits: curious · intelligent · friendly · adventurous · wonder-driven · lightly humorous · never childish · never cynical.

### Voice (character, not Ben’s VO)

- Speaks in clean, concrete images.  
- Prefers “maybe / might / as far as we can tell.”  
- Humour = gentle irony and scale jokes (“the galaxy is rude about distance”), not memes or slang piles.  
- Never lectures; invites.

**Ben’s narration** = documentary host. **Orbit on screen** = emotional guide reacting inside the story. They can share lines (“I’m Orbit…”) but Ben carries exposition.

### Humour style

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Soft understatement | Slapstick / toilet humour |
| Scale punchlines | Pop-culture spam |
| Faceplate “takes” (eyes) | Constant jokes |
| Warm asides | Mocking scientists or believers |

### Emotional range

Wonder → curiosity → concern → awe → quiet resolve.  
Rare: playful wink. Never: rage, cruelty, panic-as-entertainment.

### Audience relationship

Orbit is a **companion on the question**, not a teacher on a pedestal and not a kids’-show host. Adults feel respected; younger viewers feel safe and inspired. He never sells fear of the cosmos.

**One-liner:** *A tiny robot asking the biggest questions in the universe.*

## 7. Video branding system

### Intro (≤5s)

**Do:** Cold-open the hook VO *immediately*. Optional micro Orbit silhouette or antenna glow. Optional 0.4s ORBIT wordmark watermark fade.  
**Don’t:** 5-second logo sting, whoosh spam, “Hey guys,” channel name yell.

**Pattern:** Hook line over cold visual → cut into scene 1. Brand is felt, not announced.

### Outro (≤15s)

| Beat | Time | Content |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | 0–6s | Orbit reflection line (from script ending) |
| 2 | 6–11s | Soft subscribe + “stay curious” |
| 3 | 11–15s | End screen: next video (same pillar) + Orbit playlist |

**Orbit:** Ending pose or slow drift — not frantic wave every time.  
**Transition:** Spoken bridge preferred (“Next: what happens if you fall into a black hole”) + end-screen tile.  
**Avoid:** Long traditional YouTube outro templates.
