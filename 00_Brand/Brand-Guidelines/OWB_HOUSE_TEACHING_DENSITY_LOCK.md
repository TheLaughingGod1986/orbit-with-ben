## Rule

Whenever a **name, place, or idea** needs explaining for watchers, put an **Animistry-style side label** or a **short teach card** on that beat.

- **Density:** Kurzgesagt-level explanation density — the viewer can name the thing after the beat.
- **Look:** elegant white Didot italic **side** labels, **one at a time**, 1–4 words (or a short definition card when a one-word label is not enough).
- **Not:** flat Kurzgesagt UI / poster cards / wall of type — unless Ben unlocks that look.
- **Scope:** every new HOS part and every new Orbit long/Short going forward. **Not** a mass remint of live Orbit unless Ben says remint.

## When to fire a label or teach card

Fire when VO introduces or leans on:
- a **person**
- a **place / year**
- a **tool / object**
- a **science idea**
- a **why-it-matters** turn

Skip decorative labels that do not teach. Never stack two side labels at once. Open stamp stays place+year only — not a title bumper.
