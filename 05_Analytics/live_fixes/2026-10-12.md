AGENTS.md wants one on every long, built from `SOURCES.md` rows with an exact quote. **020 has no `SOURCES.md`**: not in git, not in the Mini checkout, not on the NAS (`01_Script/` there holds only `jupiter_fall_script_master_v01.md`). So the line can't be written to the rule yet.

Proposed route:
1. A `facts` job (Gemini via `agy`): build `020…/01_Script/SOURCES.md` for the four numbers above, each with an exact quote and the URL it was copied from.
