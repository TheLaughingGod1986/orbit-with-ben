# Packaging Panel Critique: OWB 026 (`abc_v04` Distance-Hook Candidates from J1823)

## 1. Title: `How Far Is the Nearest Star?` — **8/10**
- **Critique:** Clean, 28-character search query with a familiar noun ("Star") in the first six words. Someone searching the topic will type this exact phrase, and the film pays it off directly without clickbait. In isolation, however, it is a straight textbook question with no built-in contradiction, which means the thumbnail must carry 100% of the curiosity gap.
- **Optional sharpening (if ever re-titled):** `Why the Nearest Star Would Take 75,000 Years to Reach` (not needed while the search title is locked, provided the thumbnail supplies the shock).

## 2. Thumbnail at phone size (168×94) — **B v04: 7/10 | C v04: 5/10** (vs existing **A: 7.5/10**)
- **Critique of shared `abc_v04` plate (`potw1343a` + Blue Marble Earth):**
  - Both v04 candidates correctly depict Proxima Centauri (`potw1343a`), keep the 3-word hook in a top band (`y: 34–114`) clear of the star's core, leave the bottom-right duration badge zone completely clear, and pass the colour grade gate (`saturation=132.1 / 130.6`, `near_black=18%`).
  - At 168×94, however, pasting a crisp Blue Marble Earth cutout (`190×190 px` at `x: 70–260, y: 440–630`) right beside a huge, blown-out orange-white Hubble diffraction cross (`potw1343a` upscaled `2.12×` with visible blue-purple background noise) destroys the sense of distance. Visually, Earth looks like it is sitting right next to an exploding sun rather than separated from a tiny red dwarf by an unfathomable void.
- **Critique of B v04 (`900M YEAR WALK`) — 7/10:**
  - Readable at 168×94 with strong yellow/white contrast (`900M` yellow, `YEAR WALK` white). Turning stellar distance into a human walking time creates real curiosity, but `900M` in all-caps carries a split-second UK/metric misread as "900 metres" before the eye parses `YEAR WALK`.
- **Critique of C v04 (`4.2 LIGHT YEARS`) — 5/10:**
  - Readable at 168×94, but `4.2 LIGHT YEARS` is a dry textbook statistic with zero emotional stakes or danger for a viewer scrolling on a phone.
- **Concrete rewrite (<8):**
  - Replace C v04's `4.2 LIGHT YEARS` and fix B v04's `900M` metric ambiguity and adjacent-planet look: use **`75,000 YEAR FLIGHT`** (`75,000` in yellow) or **`900M-YEAR WALK`**, shrink Earth to half its current diameter in the bottom-left corner, darken the noisy purple field between Earth and Proxima, and add a faint dotted trajectory line with a tiny gap/arrow so the 168×94 tile visually reads as *impossible separation* rather than two bodies side by side.
