1. **Does Earth read as a foreground reference, or misleadingly as a literal size/distance diagram? Does Last Star imply Earth survives to the end of stellar time? Is the bright active-star plate appropriate for red-dwarf longevity?**
   - **Misleading in all three Draft Bs.** Placing the full-lit Apollo 17 Blue Marble (`as17-148-22727`) floating on a flat navy/purple backdrop beside the subject reads as a literal side-by-side scale or orbital diagram, not a foreground vantage point.

2. **Is `STARS MISS STARS` clear enough without an absolute scientific claim?**
   - **Scientifically acceptable as thumbnail shorthand, but verbally clumsy and repetitive.** In the Milky Way–Andromeda merger, the stellar mean free path is so vast that expected direct star-star collisions outside the central nuclei are near zero, so a 3-word cover hook does not need a hedge like "usually". However, repeating the noun (`STARS MISS STARS`) reads awkwardly and repeats the words of Title B (*Why Do Stars Usually Miss When Galaxies Collide?*). A sharper, non-repetitive 3-word contradiction hook is **`ZERO STARS CRASH`** or **`NO STARS HIT`**.
3. **Does `SATURN TO STARLIGHT` communicate the compilation's range and countdown, or look like another Saturn-only episode?**
   - **It looks like a Saturn-only episode.** At 168×94, 95% of the visual weight is Saturn (`PIA06193`, graded to saturation 145.4 so the sunlit globe blows out into flat neon yellow), and the hook starts with the word **`SATURN`**. A viewer scanning the feed will assume this is another episode about Saturn's rings (colliding with 021), not a 48-minute five-part cosmic countdown. Moreover, `SATURN TO STARLIGHT` violates `THUMBNAIL_AND_TITLE_RULES.md` §1.5 and §2.B.1 ("No poetic images or vague stakes").
4. **Does Andromeda stay a clear object rather than galaxy clutter?**
   - **The spiral disc itself is recognizable, but the uncropped field of coarse yellow/blue foreground stars around `PIA15416` creates rectangular clutter** with a visible hard edge on the left and top-left against the `#081740` canvas, plus a dark diagonal cutout notch under the bright blue star at 7 o'clock near Earth.
5. **Title alternatives — search wording, factual scope, and similarity to other live titles:**
   - **REXYxuLOBoI Title B (*Why Will Red Dwarfs Be the Last Stars?*):** Clean search question (38 chars), accurate scope, distinct from live longs, though it drops the "what happens when it dies" payoff that hooks deep-time viewers.
   - **ojk-dfOpAmw Title B (*Why Do Stars Usually Miss When Galaxies Collide?*):** Drops both high-intent search nouns (**"Milky Way"** and **"Andromeda"**), weakens the contradiction hook with the hedge word **"Usually"**, and collides too closely with the existing 019 Short title *Why Stars Don't Crash When Galaxies Meet* (`xQlV9G9lqLI`, Rule §1.6: never duplicate an existing public title's hook).
   - **m09jUdwC5hk Title B (*How Long Do Moons, Rings and Stars Last?*):** Clearer concrete nouns than "Anything in Space" (40 chars), though "Moons, Rings and Stars" reads slightly like a three-item catalogue instead of a single countdown hook.
