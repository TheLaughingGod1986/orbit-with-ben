## Mandatory Verification: `BABY GALAXIES` vs. JWST Film (`004` Scene 04), Live Title, Two-Component `JADES-GS-z14-0` Loupe, and Spacecraft Story Insets

1. **`BABY GALAXIES` vs. Scene 04 (`jwst_script_master_v01.md` lines 143–150) and Retained Title (`JWST Found Galaxies That Shouldn't Exist Yet`) — FAIL (`3 of 3` unanimous):**
   - **Script verification:** Scene 04 (`jwst_script_master_v01.md` lines 143–150) states: *"Some of the galaxies Webb confirmed are luminous when the universe was only about **three hundred million years** old. That's young. Cosmically young. And yet they shine. Some look bigger, brighter, more grown-up than many models expected for that chapter of time. [ORBIT VO:] …How are you already… you?"*
   - **Young universe vs. galaxy maturity/brightness (`3/3` agree):** All three critics verify that `~300 million years` is the **age of the universe** when the light was emitted (not a measured stellar age showing an infant proto-galaxy), and the entire scientific contradiction of Scene 04 is that inside a cosmically young universe, Webb confirmed galaxies that already look unexpectedly **luminous, bright, and grown-up** relative to model expectations. Calling them `BABY GALAXIES` conflates the young epoch of the universe with the state of the galaxies, loses the young-universe vs. mature/bright-galaxy contrast, and weakens the curiosity gap (finding "baby galaxies" in a young universe sounds expected, not like galaxies that *"Shouldn't Exist Yet"*).
   - **Title word repetition (`3/3` agree):** `BABY GALAXIES` (with `GALAXIES` as the yellow hook word) directly repeats the noun `Galaxies` from the retained live title `JWST Found Galaxies That Shouldn't Exist Yet` (`THUMBNAIL_AND_TITLE_RULES.md` §2.3: *"The thumb adds to the title. It never repeats it"*).

2. **Plural `BABY GALAXIES` vs. Two-Component `JADES-GS-z14-0` Cutout in the Single Loupe — FAIL (`3 of 3` unanimous):**
   - **Origin/loupe link & resampling honesty (`3/3` PASS):** Removing the synthetic origin brightening at `(468, 434, r=34)` makes the wide-field `JADES` NIRCam plate (`-28°` roll) honest to the NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI release, and enlarging the native `64 px` cutout to the `440 px` loupe at `(950, 373)` (`34.375%` of frame width, ≥⅓ rule `2.A.4`; bottom at `y=593`, clearing the `y=600` duration badge) is an accurate **`6.875×` linear pixel resize** with no false `4.5×` claim and no fabricated detail.
   - **Two-component ambiguity inside the loupe (`3/3` FAIL):** The `JADES-GS-z14-0` cutout contains **two adjacent sources**: the redder lower component (`JADES-GS-z14-0` at $z=14.32$, `<300 Myr` after the Big Bang, explicitly pointed out in NASA's official [JADES-GS-z14-0 pullout](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/jades-gs-z14-0-pullout-nircam/)) and an adjacent bluer/upper foreground neighbour galaxy in projection. Because `ziKBPJ6FY0U_B_v07.jpg` terminates the white arrow at the outer rim of the loupe and places a **plural** label (`BABY GALAXIES`) over a single circle containing two unresolved blobs, a phone viewer is primed to misread both components in the circle as two identified early galaxies (`codex.md`, `gemini.md`, `cursor.md`).

3. **NASA Cassini (`T037`) and Webb (`T039`) Story-Character Insets (`THUMBNAIL_AND_TITLE_RULES.md` §2.A.5) — PASS (`3 of 3` agree on non-scale framing & silhouette recognition; micro-labels unreadable at `168×94`):**

   - At `168×94` on `PHONE_SHEET_v02.jpg`, Cassini's gold foil bus + white high-gain dish (with long boom tips cropped) and Webb's gold honeycomb primary mirror + silver sunshield silhouette are recognizable (`3/3` agree), while the tiny `CASSINI` and `WEBB` text labels inside the bottom of the inset boxes collapse into an unreadable ~2 px smudge at `168×94`.
