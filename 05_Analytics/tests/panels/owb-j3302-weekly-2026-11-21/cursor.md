### Scores & Concrete Rewrites

1. **Title:** Live **6/10** · Round 2 Title B **7/10**
   - Live buries the main search noun `Europa` at word 8 and uses awkward title case (`The`, `Of`). Round 2 Title B fixes search front-loading (`Europa` at word 1, 54 chars) but stitches two clauses with a colon (`Europa's Hidden Ocean:` + `Could Life Exist…`) and uses the flat verb `Exist` instead of a survival/stakes verb. It also collides directly with Live A (`A HIDDEN OCEAN`) if ever run before Live A is replaced.
   - **Rewrite:** `Europa's Hidden Ocean: Could Life Survive Under the Ice?` (56 chars) — or if paired with `2× EARTH'S WATER` to avoid `Ocean`/`Water` conceptual overlap: `Europa: Could Life Survive Under Miles of Ice?` (46 chars).

2. **Thumbnail at phone size (`168×94`):** Live A **4/10** · Proposed B **6/10**
   - Live A is flat, washed-out grey on muddy Jupiter; dead at `168×94`.
   - Proposed B fixes legibility and contrast: `2× EARTH'S WATER` with yellow `2×` is crisp at `168×94`, sits cleanly off the subject in the top band, and leaves the `9:19` duration badge in blank lower-right space.
   - **Hard flaws on Proposed B:**
     - **Scale inversion breaks the hook:** Europa is rendered ~4× bigger than Earth, with a big arrow pointing from Earth's side onto a giant red-veined globe. At `168×94`, viewers who do not instantly recognize Galileo's Europa read it as a huge Mars-like world dwarfing Earth. The actual shock of `2× EARTH'S WATER` is that a **tiny moon smaller than our Moon** holds twice Earth's oceans; making Europa huge erases the paradox.
     - **Cheap diagram look:** The bright electric-blue radial gradient and thick blunt cartoon arrow look like a PowerPoint diagram, not space photography. The arrow points vaguely at Europa's smooth upper-left limb rather than a specific fissure or plume (§2.B.4 asks for a thin white arrow on a specific feature).
     - **Template clone with `T043`:** Same lower-left Apollo 17 Earth + same right-side orange/red sphere + same synthetic blue gradient.
   - **Rewrite:** Replace the synthetic blue gradient with deep graded cosmic dark-navy/starfield space (near-black ≤25%), shrink Europa below Earth's size (or show a fractured ice-crust close-up with a bright cyan subsurface ocean rift glowing beneath it instead of a tiny Earth inset), and replace the chunky straight arrow with a thin tapered white arrow pointing straight into a glowing ice crack.

3. **Title + thumbnail together:** Round 1 pair (Live Title + B) **8/10** · Round 2 Title B + B **7/10**
   - Round 1 pairing works well: zero repeated words (`Could Life Exist Under The Ice Of Europa?` + `2× EARTH'S WATER`); the thumb supplies the concrete numerical shock that makes "life under the ice" believable. Round 2 (`Hidden Ocean` + `WATER`) is mildly redundant conceptually.
   - **Rewrite (for Round 2):** `Europa: Could Life Survive Under Miles of Ice?` + `2× EARTH'S WATER`.

1. **Title:** Live **7/10** · Round 2 Title B **7/10**
   - Live Title hits the exact high-volume search phrase (44 chars, clean question), but is generic in browse/suggested because dozens of channels share the exact phrasing.
   - Round 2 Title B brings in the strong `7 Years` vs `1` concrete number, but `Travel Near Light Speed:` reads like a clipped fragment rather than natural phrasing, and `7 Years in 1` drops the second unit (`1` what — 1 day? 1 hour? 1 year?).    - **Rewrite:** `Why 1 Year Near Light Speed Ages Earth 7 Years` (46 chars — unambiguous units, front-loads the paradox, keeps `Light Speed` and `Earth`, no colon stutter).

2. **Thumbnail at phone size (`168×94`):** Live A **6/10** · Proposed B **3/10** (**DO NOT SHIP AS-IS**)
   - Live A breaks §2.B.2 (6 tokens / 2 lines) and uses a generic CGI ship, **yet it still beats Proposed B** because its lighting is cohesive, its space speed reads immediately, and `YOU: 1 YEAR / EARTH: 7 YEARS` delivers an actual numerical punch.
   - **Hard flaws on Proposed B:**
     - **Amateur MS-Paint rays:** The straight, aliased 1–2 px radial lines coming out of a flat airbrushed cyan circle look crude and fake at both full size and `168×94`, clashing badly with the muddy magenta haze on the left.
     - **Poor subject-ground separation:** Bruce McCandless's bright white spacesuit and legs sit directly over bright pale-blue Earth clouds; at `168×94` his lower half melts into the horizon, and full-size cutout fringes show around his MMU pack.
     - **Wrong visual story:** Everyone recognizes McCandless lazily floating above Earth in low orbit — it reads as an ISS/spacewalk video, NOT 99% of the speed of light.
     - **Cliché, weaker hook (`YOU AGE SLOWER`):** Every viewer interested in relativity already knows you age slower. Replacing the specific `1 YEAR` vs `7 YEARS` gap with generic `YOU AGE SLOWER` kills the curiosity gap.
     - *(Only positives: top text band is crisp at `168×94`, `8:55` badge is clear, and synthetic gradients pass the automated `sat`/`near_black` gate — showing the gate cannot catch bad compositing.)*
   - **Rewrite:** Put a real NASA astronaut or spacecraft viewport against **deep dark space** (no bright daylight clouds swallowing the suit), render smooth photographic radial star-streak motion blur (not aliased straight line segments) into a bright forward blue-white relativistic aberration point, and change the hook to **`1 YR = 7 YRS`** (yellow `7 YRS`) or **`7× SLOWER TIME`** (yellow `7×`) — 3 tokens max, concrete number, instant curiosity gap.

   - **Rewrite:** `What Happens If You Travel Near Light Speed?` + **`1 YR = 7 YRS`** (or **`EARTH AGES 7×`**).
