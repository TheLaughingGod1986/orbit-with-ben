# Packaging critique — J2374 three draft Bs

13 November 2026. Cursor.

Looked at the repo copies (the phone sheet, each full draft, and each live thumb at 168×94), plus `2026-11-09_DESIGN_REVIEW.md`. No other critic's file. Descriptions and chapter lists were not in the pack, so item 5 is unscored on all three. No YouTube change.

**Moon hook, the fact asked for:** `NO MORE ECLIPSES` is not fair as written. It needs a word. The film's own script says total solar eclipses give way to annular rings of fire, and alignments continue (`moon_leaving_script_master_v01.md`: "There will still be alignments… many eclipses become rings of fire — annular"). Lunar eclipses are untouched. The opening already asks about "the last perfect eclipse", not the end of eclipses. A thumb that says every eclipse stops is a claim this film cannot pay off.

Rewrite the hook to **TOTALITY ENDS** (yellow `ENDS`). Two words, same payoff, true.
