## What this changes in how we pick films (all films, not just these)
1. Start from a phrase people search (5k+ a month, competition under 50 where possible), then find our engineering or logistics question inside it.
2. Put the searched phrase in the title, and never mirror the top-ranking video's title.
3. Every long film gets two or three engineering Shorts on the same subject, linked to the long film, aired in the days before it.
4. A small channel that broke out on the subject in the last year is the proof the opening is real; no outlier means no opening.
