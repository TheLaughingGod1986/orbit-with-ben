# What works on our channels

Ben, 21 Oct 2026: "we should audit our channels and videos weekly and work out how to get better metrics to get YouTube to serve us to a bigger pool, understand what works and use that knowledge to improve."

The weekly audits (`owb-weekly-audit`, `hos-weekly-audit` in `jobs/backlog.json`) add a row to the Shorts lab for every Short public 48 h or more, and turn the evidence into rules or tests below. A rule proven on 3 or more Shorts goes to the Chief for the Shorts brief and the playbook.

**What YouTube rewards on a Short** (targets):
- **Stayed to watch** (Studio: *Viewed vs. swiped away*), decided in the first 1-2 s: 70% or more.
- **Average % viewed**: 70% or more; over 100% means people rewatch. On a 25 s Short, 44% means the average viewer left at about 11 s.
- Both decide whether the Short is shown to the next, larger pool of viewers.
