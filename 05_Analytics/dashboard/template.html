Are older videos still being watched?

How this is measured

Older = published at least 30 days before the last snapshot interval began. The counts below compare public video views between the last two snapshots. Days without uploads use YouTube Analytics calendar days (Pacific time) and the current public catalog; missing or formerly public uploads can affect this comparison. This measures views on older videos, not returning viewers.

Path to monetization

Watch time, pace and eligibility

Ad revenue requires 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, followed by YouTube review. Public view counts and Analytics hours are not the Earn balance. The lower 500-subscriber entry tier offers some features, not full ad revenue. YouTube eligibility rules.
