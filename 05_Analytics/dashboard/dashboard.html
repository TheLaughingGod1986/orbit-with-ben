How this is measured

Age is measured from the publication timestamp to the latest snapshot, using a strict over-48-hour cutoff. View gains compare public counts between the last two snapshots; for newly qualifying videos, that interval can include views from their first 48 hours. This is not live viewing or a count of returning viewers. All matching videos in the recorded public catalog are included, with longs and Shorts together. Missing comparisons stay unknown; zero or negative changes do not prove nobody watched, because YouTube can adjust counts.

Path to monetization

Watch time, pace and eligibility

Ad revenue requires 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, followed by YouTube review. Public view counts and Analytics hours are not the Earn balance. The lower 500-subscriber entry tier offers some features, not full ad revenue. YouTube eligibility rules.
