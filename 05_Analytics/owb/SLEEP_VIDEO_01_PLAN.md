## 2. Per-Film In/Out Points, Dropped Segments & Orbit Replacements

Every film is trimmed on clean sentence boundaries verified against the Whisper/alignment word JSONs (`02_Voiceover/vo_words.json` / `07_Edit-Project/vo_words_full.json`) and assembly scripts (`assemble_*.py`).

### Universal Drop Rules Applied to All 15 Films
1. **Cold-Open Hook Music Hits Dropped:** We do not use the baked stereo mix of any source film. Instead, we pull each film's **clean narration stem** (`02_Voiceover/*.wav` or `parts/*.wav`) so all opening stings, risers, and music hits are 100% absent. On earlier films (`005`, `006`, `013`, `019`, `020`) that open with a `0:00–0:03` visual/audio hook hit, picture and audio start at `0:03.00`.
2. **Agenda / Meta-Promise Lines Dropped:** Every `"In this film, I will show you..."` / `"Stay with me, and you'll see..."` agenda block (typically `~0:21–0:42`) is excised so the narration flows seamlessly as pure documentary storytelling.
3. **Mid-Film `[SUBSCRIBE BEAT]` Dropped:** Both the spoken subscribe sentence (`"We make one of these every week. Subscribing is how the next one finds you."` / `"New space mysteries every Sunday..."`) and the visual red subscribe pill overlay are completely removed.
4. **End Screens, Book Plugs & "Next Video" CTAs Dropped:** Every closing `"Next week..."` teaser, book plug (`005`), and 15–20 s end-screen card hold is trimmed immediately after the final substantive narration word (leaving a `1.5 s` natural room breath into the inter-film crossfade).
5. **Orbit Reaction Beats Dropped / Replaced with Real NASA/ESA Footage:** Wherever an `[ORBIT ACTS]` / Omni robot clip sits under ongoing narration, the narration is kept intact while the video track is covered by a real NASA/ESA/ESO plate from that film's `03_Footage/` pool. Where a brief standalone line explicitly names Orbit (e.g. `025` at `7:31.88–7:32.94` `"What would Orbit need?"`), that 1.1 s phrase is cleanly excised on the sentence pause.
