## 1. The Film Concept & Structure (`~10 Minutes`, 8 Planets, 60–75 Seconds Each)

- **Format:** One newly scripted, continuously narrated **~10-minute (`560–600 s`, `~1,350 words`)** long-form tour covering **all 8 planets in order from the Sun (`Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune`)**, giving **60 to 75 seconds (`160–175 words`)** to each planet.
- **Not a Shorts compilation (`05_Analytics/LEARNINGS.md` Rule 9 enforced):** Written as one cohesive script and recorded as one continuous voiceover take with smooth transitions outward from `0.4 AU` (`Mercury`) to `30 AU` (`Neptune`).
- **0:00–0:05 Cold Open (Zero Agenda Line, Zero Channel Intro):**
  - **First planet (`Mercury`) is on screen by `0:05`** (`Frame 0` = real NASA MESSENGER global colour mosaic `PIA19411` rotating in stark sunlight, with `MERCURY · 01 / 08 · 58 MILLION KM FROM THE SUN` lower-third).
  - **No meta-agenda line** (*"In this video we'll explain every planet…"* is banned per `J2428`). Instead, `0:00–0:05` opens straight on the completeness promise + Mercury's hook fact: *"Eight worlds orbit our Sun—and the closest one, Mercury, is shrinking while hiding water ice inside craters that never see the Sun."*
- **Why this fixes our `0:30` and full-film retention drop:**
  - Every **60 to 75 seconds**, the planet, the dominant colour palette, the real NASA spacecraft mission, and the central physical mystery **reset completely**. Before a viewer's attention can drift, the next planet arrives on screen with one striking real spacecraft image and one concrete physical number.
