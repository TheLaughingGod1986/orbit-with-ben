## What's different about it

1. **The first second: rule 8, the new style.**
   - v13a opens on the tumbling rock and says the subject's name at 0.08 s: "Apophis will fly closer than some of our satellites."
   - The older v05 opened "An asteroid will fly…" and then "Stay, and I'll show you how close."
   - The two 29 Oct Reels were the first rule-8 Shorts on Facebook. Both beat every older Reel: Apophis by about 400x, Venus by about 10x.
2. **A perfect loop.** The last line, "Half the world can look up and watch", runs straight back into "Apophis will fly closer…". The 236% average viewed on YouTube is the best we've ever had; our usual is 60-76%. On Facebook, replays are watch time, and watch time gets a Reel pushed to more people.
3. **The topic.** A real, dated event that concerns everyone on Earth (Friday the 13th, 2029), with a scare in sentence 1 and the relief inside 13 s ("It will miss"), plus something the viewer can do ("look up and watch"). It's fear, then reassurance, then participation, with no expertise needed.
4. **Short and dense:** 18.8 s, one number anyone can picture (closer than our satellites), and a scale bar.

## A natural experiment we can read

Instagram and Threads got the OLD v05 cut on 28 Oct (Buffer can't swap media on a sent post), while Facebook got v13a on 29 Oct. **Ben: Apophis's Instagram views would tell us how much was the new opening versus Facebook itself.**

## Caveats

- This is one Reel. Facebook virality has a lot of luck in it, so treat it as a strong hint, not a law.
- Only 2 of 214K viewers clicked through to YouTube.
- The Reels post as "Untitled Video" (J1277 is fixing that).
