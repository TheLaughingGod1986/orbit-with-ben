- **Mitigation:** Do **not** alter `hos-search-research` or HOS's standing *"no space"* rule. Only unlock a 2nd pair if the 14 Feb readout PASSes.

### 2. Audience & Visual-Style Mismatch (HOS 3D Cartoon vs Orbit Real NASA/ESA Imagery)
- **Risk:** HOS is warm period 3D cartoon animation (`Education`, cat `27`, 7–9 min, previously 86% UK / 2.5% US pre-relaunch); Orbit is real NASA/ESA space photography + dark-navy code graphics (`Science & Tech`, cat `28`, 8–10+ min, or Orbit's 10 Jan illustrated-style test). Viewers clicking across may bounce in the first 15 s if the shift in tone/visuals surprises them, dragging down `0:30` retention (already "lower" on most longs on both channels). Additionally, with 19 and 6 subscribers today, existing audience cross-pollination is negligible unless search/Shorts bring cold viewers first.
- **Mitigation:**
  - Every film **stands 100% alone** (never assume the viewer saw the sister video).
  - Put cross-channel links at **~65%+ (one Info Card)** and the **end (handoff line + End Screen)** — never inside the fragile `0:00–0:60` hook window.
  - Set visual expectation explicitly in the spoken handoff (*"Over on our sister space channel Orbit With Ben, see the real NASA spacecraft images from inside Neptune's clouds"*).
  - Note: if Orbit's **10 Jan 2027 illustrated-style test** (`studio/plan.json`) wins, the visual gap between OWB and HOS narrows naturally.
