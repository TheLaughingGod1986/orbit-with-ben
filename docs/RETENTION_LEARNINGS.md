## Snapshot — 2026-08-04 (channel ~1 week)

**Traffic:** Shorts Feed dominates (~116 of ~146 views). Long ~5 views. Subs: 0.

### Shorts

| Pattern | Evidence | Learning |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Opening cliff | Retention drops hardest in first ~8–10s; stayed-to-watch ~20% on one Short | **P0 = 0–1.5s hook** — no setup, no logo, no soft wonder wind-up |
| Survivors stay | Curve flattens after early drop | Topic often viable; **execution of open** fails |
| Length vs AVD | ~44s Short → ~19s AVD · ~44–51% viewed | Package **22–30s**; don’t pad |
| Completion ~21% | End retention low on longer cuts | Payoff earlier; soft CTA last 1–2s only |
| Zero subs | Views without follow | Contextual “follow for next mystery” — never interrupt hook |

### Long-form

| Pattern | Evidence | Learning |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Cold long | ~5 views | Needs Shorts → Related funnel; don’t overreact |
| Runtime | JWST ~16 min in flight | Prefer **8–12 min** for next few after JWST ships |

### Do / Don’t (locked)

**Do:** punch-first VO · first-frame drama · visual change ≤3s · Orbit in-story · soft CTA · VO–picture lock · pre-build vidIQ · 10–12 min longs (V013+) · chapter teach-points · Related pill Shorts funnel (no new Short pins)  
**Don’t:** welcome intros · fearbait titles · 45–60s stretched Shorts · schedule thrash · niche pivot on &lt;1k views · generic B-roll under specific VO  

**Going forward (full list):** `00_Brand/Channel-Setup/RETENTION_AND_GROWTH_LOCKED.md`
