Why does Uranus have 21-year winters, and what did Voyager 2 find when it flew past a planet knocked onto its side?

Follow the story of the seventh planet from the Sun — from William Herschel's 1781 telescope discovery and the 1977 airborne occultation that revealed its vertical rings, to Voyager 2's six-hour encounter in January 1986 and the 2007 equinox storms.

Explore an ice giant whose atmosphere drops to minus 224.2 degrees Celsius, whose magnetic field twists into a giant corkscrew, and whose fractured moon Miranda hides fault canyons up to twelve times as deep as the Grand Canyon.
