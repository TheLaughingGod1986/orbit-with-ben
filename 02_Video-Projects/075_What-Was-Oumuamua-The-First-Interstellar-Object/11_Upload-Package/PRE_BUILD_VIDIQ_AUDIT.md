1. **The Dot Going Too Fast for the Sun to Keep (`October 2017`)** — On **19 October 2017** (`WHEN: 19 October 2017`), astronomer **Robert Weryk** (`WHO: Robert Weryk (born 1981) — Pan-STARRS astronomer`) spotted a faint point of light in Hawaii's `1.8-m` `Pan-STARRS1` telescope just `0.2 AU` from Earth. Tracing its path backward to its **9 September 2017** perihelion (`WHEN: 9 September 2017`) showed it had whipped around the Sun at **`87.71 km/s` (`315,750 km/h`)** on a hyperbolic orbit with **eccentricity `e = 1.201`** (`DATA` #1 + `SCALE: 87.7 km/s peak speed, ~3x Earth's 29.8 km/s orbital speed; e = 1.201 vs e < 1.0 for every bound comet`), arriving from the direction of Lyra at `26.33 km/s` (`FACT: Eccentricity 1.201 = unbound`).
2. **Ten-to-One Brightness Swings: Cigar or Pancake? (`November 2017`)** — Leading an emergency global campaign with the `8.2-m` **Very Large Telescope (`VLT`)** and **Hubble**, planetary astronomer **Karen Meech** (`WHO: Karen Meech (born 1959) — University of Hawaii astronomer`, `Meech et al. 2017`) found `'Oumuamua` dimmed and brightened by a factor of **`10:1` (`2.5 magnitudes`) every `7.3–8.67 hours`** (`DATA` #2 + `SCALE: 10:1 brightness swing vs <2:1 for almost all solar-system asteroids`) as it tumbled end-over-end — pointing to a **`~230-m` cigar or a `~115 × 111 × 19 m` (`6:1`) oblate pancake** (`Mashchenko 2019`).
3. **The Paradox: Accelerating `40,000 km` Ahead With Zero Tail (`2018`)** — While `33 hours` of **Spitzer Space Telescope** infrared observations (`Trilling et al. 2018`) detected **zero dust coma and zero `CO` or `CO₂` gas**, **Marco Micheli** (`WHO: Marco Micheli (born 1983) — ESA planetary astronomer`, `Micheli et al. 2018`) proved in **June 2018** (`WHEN: June 2018`) that `'Oumuamua` was accelerating away from the Sun at **`4.92 × 10⁻⁶ m/s²` (`30σ`)**, pulling **`~40,000 km` ahead** of a purely gravitational trajectory (`DATA` #3 + `SCALE: 40,000 km offset = more than 3x Earth's 12,742-km diameter`, `FACT: 40,000 km ahead of gravity`).
4. **How Science Solved It in `2023`: Cosmic Rays & Trapped Hydrogen (`Bergner & Seligman 2023`)** — Why nitrogen-iceberg, pure-hydrogen-iceberg, and solar-sail models struggled, and how in **March 2023** (`WHEN: March 2023`) **Jennifer Bergner** (`WHO: Jennifer Bergner (born 1992) — UC Berkeley astrochemist`) and **Darryl Seligman** (`WHO: Darryl Seligman (born 1992) — Cornell astrophysicist`) proved in *Nature* (`615`, `610–613`) that galactic cosmic rays striking amorphous water ice over hundreds of millions of years convert up to `20–35%` of water into trapped **molecular hydrogen (`H₂`)** — which gently escaped as sunlight warmed `'Oumuamua`'s outer metres, pushing the small `~115-m` body outward with zero visible dust (`FACT: Cosmic rays made trapped H2`).
