| Claim in the film | Source |
|---|---|
| Surface about 465 °C, pressure about 92 bar | NASA NSSDCA Venus Fact Sheet: average temperature 737 K (464 °C); surface pressure 92 bars. https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/venusfact.html |
| Atmosphere mostly carbon dioxide, with sulfuric-acid clouds | NASA Venus Facts: atmosphere mostly CO₂; dense clouds of sulfuric acid; pressure ~93× Earth sea level. https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/ |
| Runaway greenhouse made Venus the hottest planet | Same NASA page: thick atmosphere traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect, hottest surface in the solar system aside from the Sun. |
| Early Venus **may have** been habitable with liquid water (open) | Way, M. J. et al. (2016). Was Venus the first habitable world of our solar system? *Geophys. Res. Lett.*, 43, 8376–8383. Quote: “At its current rotation period, Venus’s climate could have remained habitable until at least 0.715 Gya.” NASA summary: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/climate-change/nasa-climate-modeling-suggests-venus-may-have-been-habitable/ |
| Oceans **may never** have condensed (open counter) | Turbet, M. et al. (2021). Day–night cloud asymmetry prevents early oceans on Venus but not on Earth. *Nature*, 598, 276–280. DOI [10.1038/s41586-021-03873-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03873-w). Line: nightside water clouds have a strong net warming effect that “inhibits surface water condensation” — their model concludes oceans never formed. Film says **“may have”**, never “had”. |
| Phosphine in the clouds (2020) is disputed | Omit from the film, or label disputed if mentioned. Cool Worlds / peer debate cites Greaves et al. and abiotic alternatives; not a locked claim for Orbit. |
| DAVINCI will sample the atmosphere on descent and image Alpha Regio | NASA DAVINCI: Deep Atmosphere Venus Investigation of Noble gases, Chemistry, and Imaging — spacecraft + probe; probe samples chemistry, T/P/winds, then images Alpha Regio below the clouds. https://science.nasa.gov/mission/davinci/ |
| VERITAS maps topography, rock types, and how Venus diverged from Earth | NASA VERITAS (Venus Emissivity, Radio Science, InSAR, Topography, and Spectroscopy) orbiter. https://science.nasa.gov/mission/veritas/ |
| EnVision (ESA) studies Venus from core to upper atmosphere | ESA EnVision: next Venus orbiter, holistic view from inner core to upper atmosphere; targeting Nov 2031; NASA provides VenSAR. https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Envision/Envision_factsheet · https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/envision |
