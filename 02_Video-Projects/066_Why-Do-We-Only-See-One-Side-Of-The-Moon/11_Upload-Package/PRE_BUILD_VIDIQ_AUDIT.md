**Chapter list after audit** (5 acts, each with a teach-point + `Teach on screen` markers):

1. **The Face That Never Turns Away** — Why every human in history has looked up across `238,855 miles (384,400 km)` (`30 Earth diameters`) and seen the exact same dark plains, and why calling the hidden half the "dark side" is wrong (`New Moon` = full blazing sunlight on the far side at up to `390 K / +240 °F` vs `95 K / -290 °F` at night; `DATA` + `SCALE`).
2. **Does the Moon Actually Spin?** — If the Moon didn't rotate at all, Earth would see every side of it over a month; instead, the Moon spins once on its axis in `27.3 Earth days` (`655.7 hours`) and orbits Earth in `27.3 Earth days` (`29.5 days` relative to the Sun; `FACT` + animated spin-orbit clock).
3. **How Earth's Gravity Locked the Moon's Spin** — How Earth's gravity stretched the hot, young Moon into a slight football bulge and pulled that bulge back whenever the Moon spun ahead, dissipating heat until its spin locked (`synchronous tidal locking` — just like every large moon in the Solar System; `DATA` diagram).
4. **1959: The Shock on the Other Side** — How Soviet spacecraft **Luna 3** (`October 1959`, `WHEN: 1959`) and **Apollo 8** (`December 1968`) revealed a battered world of bright highlands with almost none of the dark basaltic seas (`maria`) that cover the near side.
5. **Why One Side Flooded With Lava and the Other Didn't** — How **NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)** and **GRAIL** mapped the Moon's interior (`149-mile / 240-km` solid iron inner core, `56-mile / 90-km` liquid iron outer shell) and proved the near-side crust is `25 miles (40 km)` thick while the far-side crust is up to `37 miles (60 km)` thick — so giant impacts between `4.2` and `1.2 billion years ago` cracked open magma floods on our side while the thicker far-side shield stayed sealed (`DATA` comparison).
