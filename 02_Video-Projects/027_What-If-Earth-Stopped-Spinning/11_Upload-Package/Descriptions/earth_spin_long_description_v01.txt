Right now, in Britain, the ground is carrying you east at about a thousand kilometres an hour, and you feel nothing. What if Earth stopped spinning? A sudden stop is a thought experiment: the air would keep going. The real story is that Earth is already slowing down, and the clocks have been keeping score.

The speed you cannot feel, then what a sudden stop would do to the air, what a slow stop would do to the seas and the length of a day, and how the Moon is already braking the spin. Fossil corals, ancient eclipses and leap seconds are how we know the day has been changing.
