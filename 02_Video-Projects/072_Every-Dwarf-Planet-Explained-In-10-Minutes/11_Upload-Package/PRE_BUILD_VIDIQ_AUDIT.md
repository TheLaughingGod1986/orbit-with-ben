**Chapter list after audit** (5 chapters covering all 5 official dwarf planets + frontier candidates, each with a teach-point + `Teach on screen` markers):

1. **Why 9 Planets Became 8 (And Created the 5 Dwarf Planets, `2005–2006`)** — On **5 January 2005** (`WHEN: 5 January 2005`), **Mike Brown** (`WHO: Mike Brown (born 1965) — Caltech planetary astronomer`), Chad Trujillo, and David Rabinowitz spotted **Eris** beyond Neptune. When Hubble and Keck (`Brown & Schaller 2007`) proved Eris weighs **`27%` more than Pluto** (`1.66 × 10²² kg` vs `1.303 × 10²² kg`, `DATA` #1), the International Astronomical Union voted on **24 August 2006** (`WHEN: 24 August 2006`) to require a planet to clear its orbital neighbourhood (`Earth = 1.7 million ×` its orbital debris vs `Pluto = 0.077 ×`, `FACT: Pluto is 7% of its zone`).
2. **Dwarf Planets #1 & #2: `Ceres` (`2.77 AU`) & `Pluto` (`39.5 AU`) — The Two Worlds We Have Visited Up Close** — Discovered on **1 January 1801** (`WHEN: 1 January 1801`) by **Giuseppe Piazzi** (`WHO: Giuseppe Piazzi (1746–1826) — Palermo astronomer`), **`940-km` Ceres** holds `39%` of the Asteroid Belt's mass and hides briny sodium-carbonate domes (`Dawn`, `2015`); `30×` farther out, **`2,377-km` Pluto** (discovered **18 February 1930** by **Clyde Tombaugh**, `WHO: Clyde Tombaugh (1906–1997) — Discovered Pluto in 1930`) surprised **New Horizons** (`14 July 2015`, `WHEN: 14 July 2015`) with its `1,000-km` convecting nitrogen-ice heart (`Sputnik Planitia`) and `1,212-km` binary partner **Charon**.
3. **Dwarf Planet #3: `Haumea` (`43 AU`) — The `3.9-Hour` Cosmic Rugby Ball With a Ring** — Spinning once every **`3.915 hours`** (`DATA` #2 + `SCALE: 6x faster than Earth's day`), **Haumea** (`~2,100 × 1,680 × 1,074 km`) is stretched into a triaxial ellipsoid surrounded by two moons (`Hiʻiaka` and `Namaka`) and a **`70-km`-wide ring** discovered during a **21 January 2017** stellar occultation (`WHEN: 21 January 2017`, `Ortiz et al. 2017`, `FACT: First Kuiper Belt ring`).
4. **Dwarf Planets #4 & #5: `Makemake` (`45 AU`) & `Eris` (`68–96 AU`) — What JWST Found Inside the Deep Freeze (`2024`)** — For years, **`1,430-km` Makemake** and **`2,326-km` Eris** (`albedo 0.96`, brighter than fresh snow at `-243 °C`) were assumed to be cold, inert relics. In **2024** (`WHEN: February 2024`), **James Webb Space Telescope (`NIRSpec`)** spectra (`Glein et al. 2024`; `Grundy et al. 2024`, `DATA` #3) revealed low-deuterium methane (`D/H ≈ 2.5–2.9 × 10⁻⁴`) cooked inside **`>150 °C` warm rocky cores** (`FACT: Warm cores under -243°C ice`).
5. **Beyond the Official 5: `Quaoar`'s Impossible Ring & `Sedna`'s `11,400-Year` Orbit** — Why `Quaoar` (`1,090 km`, whose `2023` ring sits at `7.4` planetary radii outside the classic Roche limit, `Morgado et al. 2023`), `Gonggong` (`1,230 km`), `Orcus` (`910 km`), and `Sedna` (`995 km`, on an `11,400-year` orbit reaching `937 AU`) prove the outer solar system holds dozens more dwarf planets waiting to be explored.
