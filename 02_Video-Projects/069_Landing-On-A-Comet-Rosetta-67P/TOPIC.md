### 2.5 Primary Scientific & Agency Fact Sheet (Pre-Verified for `SOURCES.md`)
- **Biele et al. (`2015`, *Science* `349`, `aaa9816`, *"The landing(s) of Philae and inferences about comet surface mechanical properties"*):**
  - On **12 November 2014**, ESA's `100-kg` **Philae** lander touched down on Comet `67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko` at `Agilkia` at `15:34:04 UTC` (`~1 m/s` descent speed) at `510 million km` from Earth (`3.0 AU` from the Sun). Because the cold-gas top thruster and anchor harpoons did not fire, Philae **bounced twice**: first flight lasted **`1 hour 50 minutes`** (reaching `~1 km` altitude and travelling `~1 km` across the comet), touching a crater rim at `17:25:26 UTC`, and coming to final rest at **`Abydos` at `17:31:17 UTC`** (`64 hours` of primary battery science before hibernation).
- **Sierks et al. (`2015`, *Science* `347`, `aaa1044`) & Pätzold et al. (`2016`, *Nature* `530`, `63–65`):**
  - Comet `67P` is a contact binary ("rubber duck") with dimensions **`4.3 × 2.6 × 2.1 km`** (large lobe `4.1 × 3.3 × 1.8 km`, small lobe `2.6 × 2.3 × 1.8 km`), mass **`9.982 × 10¹² kg` (`~10 billion tonnes`)**, bulk density **`533 kg/m³`** (half the density of water ice, with **`72–75%` internal porosity**), rotation period **`12.404 hours`**, and surface albedo **`~6%`** (darker than coal).
- **Altwegg et al. (`2015`, *Science* `347`, `1261952`; `2016`, *Science Advances* `2`, `e1600285`):**
  - Rosetta's `ROSINA` mass spectrometer measured a deuterium-to-hydrogen (`D/H`) ratio in `67P`'s water vapour of **`(5.3 ± 0.7) × 10⁻⁴` — more than three times (`3.4×`) Earth's ocean water (`1.5576 × 10⁻⁴`)**, showing that Jupiter-family comets like `67P` did not deliver the bulk of Earth's oceans; `ROSINA` also detected the amino acid **glycine**, phosphorus, and molecular oxygen (`O₂`).
