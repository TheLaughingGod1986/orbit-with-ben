Why is Earth's north magnetic pole racing towards Siberia, and what would a magnetic field reversal mean for the world beneath it?

Follow compass needles frozen into ancient lava, the liquid iron deep beneath our feet and the South Atlantic Anomaly, where satellites face extra radiation. A kauri tree's rings hold another clue to a time when Earth's magnetic shield grew weak.

From the air above our heads to auroras far from the poles, explore what changes when north and south trade places.
