# 029 code graphics v01 (J0347)

£0. No Flow, no Vertex, no Omni, no ElevenLabs. Built from `PLATE_BOARDS_v02.json` after the J0330 boards landed on main: 13 `route: code` plates plus the r22 subscribe card.

Renderer: `07_Edit-Project/build_code_graphics_v01.py`. Pictures: `07_Edit-Project/code_graphics_v01/` (mp4 and jpg stay out of git). SHA-256s are in `RENDER_MANIFEST.json` once the render finishes. 1920×1080, 50 fps, silent, so every board `use_s` is a whole number of frames. House type is Arial Bold. Labels are consecutive words from `02_Voiceover/words.json`, 4 words or fewer, inside the action-safe area, on screen while those words are spoken. No new claims.

Earth and Mars discs (Blue Marble, NASA PIA00407) sit on the scale graphic. The Sun is a small yellow-white dot, so the red disc is not read as Betelgeuse (Chief FIX, 16 Oct). r05_scale is text-free. Shells, the dip, the astrolabe, the teaspoon and the scroll are our own drawings. The scroll is a plain object. It is not labelled as the 1054 record. The year on screen is the year the voice says.
