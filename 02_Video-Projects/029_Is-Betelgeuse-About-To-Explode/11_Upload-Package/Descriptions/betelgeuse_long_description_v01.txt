Betelgeuse is a dying red supergiant star on the shoulder of Orion. When it dimmed by more than 60 per cent in late 2019, astronomers wondered whether it was preparing to go supernova — and what our sky will look like when it finally explodes.

Follow the VLT and Hubble observations behind the Great Dimming, the physics of core collapse, how bright a Betelgeuse supernova will shine from Earth, and what seismic models say about how much time the star has left.
