Every spacecraft humanity has ever landed on another world sits inside a bubble of light less than two light-hours wide—while the nearest star beyond our Sun is 4.24 light-years away. In this 58-minute journey film, we travel outward from the spinning ground under your feet, to the crushing 464 °C surface of Venus, the nuclear rovers of Mars, the frozen comet cloud where another star is heading our way, across the four-light-year gap to Proxima Centauri, near the speed of light itself, and 600 light-years out to the dying supergiant Betelgeuse.
