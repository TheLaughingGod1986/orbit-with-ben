### Story beats (all numbers to verify)

1. What is up there: about 420 tonnes, about 109 m across, roughly 400 km up, 16 orbits a day.
2. Why it can't stay: thin air drags it down, so it needs regular boosts; ageing structure.
3. The plan: a dedicated deorbit vehicle (NASA chose SpaceX to build it in 2024) pushes it down after operations end, planned for about 2030-2031.
4. Where: Point Nemo in the South Pacific, the spot farthest from land, about 2,700 km from the nearest coast, where other spacecraft already lie.
5. What came before: Mir (2001, controlled) and Skylab (1979, pieces over Western Australia).
6. What survives re-entry and what burns.
