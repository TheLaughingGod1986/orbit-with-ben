So how long would the journey take?

Walk at a steady pace, never stopping, never sleeping, and you would reach Proxima in about nine hundred million years.

Take a car at motorway speed, and it is about forty-six million years, far longer than humans have existed.

A jet airliner cuts it to about five million years.

Voyager 1 is the fastest thing we have ever sent out of the solar system, moving at about seventeen kilometres every second. It is not heading towards Proxima. But at its speed, crossing the same distance would take about seventy-five thousand years.

Even light, at three hundred thousand kilometres a second, needs four years and three months.

Put that beside the light you know. Moonlight reaches you in just over a second. Sunlight takes about eight minutes. Light from the nearest star takes more than four years, and that is the shortest trip of its kind there is.
