Look up on a clear night and the stars seem close, scattered just above the rooftops. So how far away is the nearest one?

Light, the fastest thing there is, takes more than four years to cross the gap. And here is the strange part. You cannot see that star at all. Not without a telescope.

In this film I will show you which star it is, how anyone measured a distance that large, what that gap looks like if you shrink the whole thing down to fit on Earth, and how long it would take to get there.
