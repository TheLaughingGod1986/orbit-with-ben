## Notes for Claude

- **All core distance, velocity, and historical numbers are VERIFIED** against ESA Gaia EDR3/DR3, NASA JPL Voyager status logs, *Nature*, *A&A*, and historical primary records (*Astronomische Nachrichten* / *MNRAS*).
- **Proxima Centauri vs Alpha Centauri naming:** Proxima Centauri (Alpha Centauri C) is the single closest star to the Sun at ~4.24 ly. The binary Alpha Centauri AB is the closest *star system* with Sun-like stars, located at ~4.37 ly (~0.13 ly further away than Proxima). Script should distinguish the individual nearest star (Proxima) from the system pair (A and B).
- **Voyager 1 destination caution:** Voyager 1 is **not** headed toward Proxima Centauri or Alpha Centauri. It is traveling toward the northern constellation Ophiuchus / Camelopardalis (passing within 1.6 light-years of the red dwarf star Gliese 445 in about 40,000 years). The spoken script line must frame the ~75,000-year travel time as "at Voyager's speed, it would take roughly 75,000 years to cross that distance," rather than implying it is en route to Proxima.
- **Proxima b radius & mass:** UNVERIFIED / OPEN. Because Proxima b was detected via radial velocity and does not transit its host star from Earth's line of sight, its true radius is unknown and its mass is constrained only as a minimum mass ($m \sin i \approx 1.17\,M_\oplus$). It must NOT be described as an "Earth twin" or "Earth-like planet" in the VO—only as an "Earth-mass planet in the temperate zone."
- **Breakthrough Starshot framing:** Must remain explicitly tagged as an *unbuilt proposed design study* / *concept plan*. No hardware is currently built or funded for launch.
- **Scale model consistency:** Grapefruit (14 cm) to pinhead (1.3 mm) at 15 m, with Proxima at 4,000 km, is exact $1 : 10^{10}$ geometry. If using fruit analogies, keep the scale factor explicit ($1\text{ cm} = 100{,}000\text{ km}$).
