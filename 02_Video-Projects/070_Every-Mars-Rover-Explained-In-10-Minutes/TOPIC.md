### 2.5 Primary Scientific & Agency Fact Sheet (Pre-Verified for `SOURCES.md`)
- **Historical Precursor — Soviet `Prop-M` (`Mars 2` & `Mars 3`, 1971, NASA NSSDCA):**
  - Two `4.5-kg` tethered ski-walking rovers (`15-m` umbilical cable, designed by `Alexander Kemurdzhian`): `Mars 2` crashed on `27 November 1971`; `Mars 3` made the first soft landing on Mars on **`2 December 1971`** in Ptolemaeus Crater during a global dust storm, but transmitted for only **`14.5 to 20 seconds`** (`70` scan lines of a blank image) before failing, so `Prop-M` was never deployed.
- **Rover #1 — `Sojourner` (`Mars Pathfinder`, NASA/JPL, `Golombek et al. 1997`, *Science* `278`, `1743–1748`):**
  - Landed **4 July 1997** in **Ares Vallis** (`19.13° N, 33.22° W`) using a `21.5-m` parachute, solid retro-rockets, and a **`24-lobe` (`4-face`) tetrahedral airbag cocoon** (`5.2 m` diameter) that bounced **`>15 times`** (`15.7 m` first bounce) across `~1 km`.
  - Mass: **`11.5 kg` (`25 lb`)**, dimensions `65 × 48 × 30 cm`, `6` aluminium `13-cm` wheels on a **rocker-bogie** suspension (`Donald Bickler`, JPL), top speed **`1 cm/s` (`0.022 mph`)**, `0.22 m²` GaAs solar panel (`~16 W` peak) + `LiSOCl₂` non-rechargeable batteries inside a silica **aerogel** Warm Electronics Box (`WEB`).
  - Planned life: **`7 sols`** (`30-sol` goal). Operated **`83 sols`** (last signal **27 September 1997** when the Pathfinder lander battery failed), drove **`~100 metres`** (`104 m`), took `550` rover images, and analysed `16` rocks/soils (`Barnacle Bill` andesite-like silica rock, `Yogi` basalt) with its `APXS`.
- **Rovers #2 & #3 — `Spirit` (`MER-A`) & `Opportunity` (`MER-B`) (`Squyres et al. 2004`, *Science* `306`; `Squyres et al. 2008`, *Science* `320`, `1063–1067`):**
  - Mass: **`185 kg` (`408 lb`)** each, `1.5 m` high × `2.3 m` wide × `1.6 m` long, solar arrays generating `~140 W` peak (`~900 Wh/sol` at start), planned for **`90 sols`** and `600 m`.
  - **Spirit (`MER-A`):** Landed **4 January 2004 UTC** in **Gusev Crater**; dragging a seized right-front wheel in **2006** scraped bright soil at **Home Plate** composed of **`~90%` opaline silica (`SiO₂·nH₂O`)** (`Squyres et al. 2008`), proving ancient hydrothermal hot springs or volcanic fumaroles; became embedded in soft sulfate sand at **Troy** in **May 2009**, drove **`7.73 km` (`4.80 miles`)**, and sent its last contact on **22 March 2010** (`Sol 2210`).
  - **Opportunity (`MER-B`):** Landed **25 January 2004 UTC** in **Meridiani Planum** (`Eagle Crater`); discovered spherical grey **hematite (`Fe₂O₃`) concretions ("blueberries")** and sulfate salts (**jarosite**) formed in acidic liquid water; wind **"cleaning events"** repeatedly restored solar output; drove **`45.16 km` (`28.06 miles` — off-Earth driving distance record)** across **`5,111 sols` (`14 years, 4 months`)** until the **June 2018** global dust storm (`tau ≈ 10.8`); last contact **10 June 2018** (`Sol 5111`).
- **Rover #4 — `Curiosity` (`Mars Science Laboratory`, `Grotzinger et al. 2014`, *Science* `343`, `1242777`):**
  - Landed **6 August 2012 UTC** in **Gale Crater** (`154 km` wide, with `5.5-km` **Mount Sharp / Aeolis Mons**) via the first rocket-powered **Sky Crane** descent stage (`7.6 m` nylon tethers).
  - Mass: **`899 kg` (`1,982 lb`)** (`3.0 × 2.7 × 2.2 m`), powered by an **`MMRTG`** containing **`4.8 kg` of `PuO₂`** (`~110 W` electrical + `~2,000 W` thermal heat at launch).
  - Fired **`ChemCam`** (`1,067 nm` laser pulses delivering `~14 MW` for `5 nanoseconds` to vaporise rock up to `7 m` away) and drilled **Yellowknife Bay** mudstone (`John Klein` and `Cumberland`, `2013`), proving a neutral-pH, low-salinity freshwater lake (`Grotzinger et al. 2014`) with clay minerals and organic carbon molecules; still active (`>4,300 sols`, `>32 km` driven).
- **Rover #5 — `Zhurong` (`Tianwen-1`, CNSA, `Liu et al. 2022`, *Science Advances* `8`, `eabn8555`; `Li et al. 2022`, *Nature* `610`, `308–312`):**
  - Landed **14 May 2021 UTC** (`15 May` CST) in southern **Utopia Planitia** (`25.066° N, 109.925° E`), making **China the 2nd country** to successfully operate a rover on Mars.
  - Mass: **`240 kg` (`529 lb`)**, `4` butterfly-wing solar panels with anti-dust coating + `n-undecane` (`C₁₁H₂₄`) phase-change thermal storage bricks, active suspension with **inchworm wheel-walking**, and a wireless drop-camera that photographed Zhurong beside its lander.
  - Drove **`1,921 metres` (`1.921 km`)** across **`347 sols`** before entering winter hibernation in **May 2022**, using dual-frequency ground-penetrating radar (`RoPeR`) to map two distinct subsurface flood-sediment layers down to `~80 metres` (`Li et al. 2022`).
- **Rover #6 — `Perseverance` (`Mars 2020`, `Farley et al. 2020`, *Space Science Reviews*; `Hoffman et al. 2022`, *Science Advances* `8`, `eabp8636`):**
  - Landed **18 February 2021** in **Jezero Crater** (`45 km` diameter ancient lake & river delta) using Sky Crane + autonomous **Terrain-Relative Navigation (`TRN`)**.
  - Mass: **`1,025 kg` (`2,260 lb`)** (`~89.1×` the mass of `11.5-kg` Sojourner), `MMRTG` nuclear power, `23` cameras, `2` microphones, `PIXL` & `SHERLOC` spectrometers, titanium **sample-return tubes** (`10-tube` depot at **Three Forks** completed `January 2023`, plus `2024` **Cheyava Falls** rock core), and deployed **`1.8-kg` Ingenuity** (`72 flights`).
  - **`MOXIE` (`Mars Oxygen In-Situ Resource Utilization Experiment`):** Pulled in Mars's `95% CO₂` atmosphere (`~5 torr`), compressed it, and split `CO₂ → CO + ½O₂` via solid-oxide electrolysis (`SOXE`) at **`800 °C`** (`Hoffman et al. 2022`); across **`16` runs** (`April 2021 – August 2023`), `MOXIE` generated **`122 grams` of breathable `O₂`** at **`98%+` purity** and up to **`12 g/hour`** (`2×` its `6 g/h` design target).
