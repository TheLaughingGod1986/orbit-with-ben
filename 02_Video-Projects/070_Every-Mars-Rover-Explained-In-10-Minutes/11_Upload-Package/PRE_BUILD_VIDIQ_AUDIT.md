**Chapter list after audit** (5 chronological acts covering all 6 rovers, each with a teach-point + `Teach on screen` markers):

1. **Rover #1: `Sojourner` (`4 July 1997`) — The `11.5-kg` Microwave That Proved Wheels Work on Mars** — After the Soviet `4.5-kg` `Prop-M` ski-rover on `Mars 3` (`2 December 1971`, `WHEN: December 1971`) lost contact after `14.5 seconds`, NASA's `Mars Pathfinder` bounced `>15 times` across Ares Vallis inside a `24-lobe` airbag cocoon (`WHEN: 4 July 1997`). **Sojourner** (`11.5 kg`, `1 cm/s` top speed, `Donald Bickler`'s `6-wheel` rocker-bogie suspension, `WHO: Donald Bickler (born 1931) — JPL rover engineer`) used silica aerogel insulation to outlast its `7-sol` plan by `12×` (`83 sols`, `100 m` driven, `DATA` #1).
2. **Rovers #2 & #3: `Spirit` & `Opportunity` (`January 2004`) — Following the Water** — Scaling up `16×` to **`185 kg` (`408 lb`)** golf-cart twins (`WHO: Steve Squyres (born 1956) — MER principal investigator` · `WHEN: January 2004`): **Spirit** (`Gusev Crater`, `7.73 km`) accidentally scraped **`90%` opaline silica** at Home Plate (`2006`, `FACT: Broken wheel found hot spring`) proving ancient hydrothermal steam vents, while **Opportunity** (`Meridiani Planum`, `45.16 km` across `5,111 sols`, `DATA` #2) discovered hematite **"blueberries"** and jarosite formed in liquid water.
3. **Rover #4: `Curiosity` (`6 August 2012`) — The `899-kg` Nuclear Chemist & the Sky Crane** — Too heavy for airbags, NASA invented the rocket-powered **Sky Crane** (`WHEN: 6 August 2012`) to lower **`899-kg` Curiosity** into **Gale Crater**. Powered day and night by **`4.8 kg` of plutonium-238 dioxide (`MMRTG`)** and firing a **`14-megawatt` pulsed laser (`ChemCam`)**, Curiosity drilled Yellowknife Bay mudstone (`2013`, `WHO: John Grotzinger (born 1957) — MSL chief scientist`) to prove a neutral-pH freshwater lake once existed on Mars.
4. **Rover #5: `Zhurong` (`14 May 2021`) — China's Butterfly-Wing Radar Explorer** — On **14 May 2021** (`WHEN: 14 May 2021`), CNSA's **`240-kg` Zhurong** landed in **Utopia Planitia**, making China the second nation to drive on Mars. Using `n-undecane` phase-change thermal windows, an inchworm active suspension, a deployable wireless selfie camera (`FACT: Wireless camera took selfie`), and dual-frequency ground-penetrating radar (`RoPeR`), Zhurong mapped buried flood-sediment layers `80 metres` deep across **`1,921 metres`** (`347 sols`).
5. **Rover #6: `Perseverance` (`18 February 2021`) — `1,025 kg`, `MOXIE` Oxygen & Mars Sample Return** — Weighing **`1,025 kg`** (`89×` Sojourner's mass, `DATA` #3 + `SCALE: Microwave oven vs SUV`), **Perseverance** touched down in **Jezero Crater** (`WHEN: 18 February 2021`), deployed the `1.8-kg` **Ingenuity** helicopter (`72 flights`), cached sealed titanium rock cores at **Three Forks**, and used **`MOXIE`** at **`800 °C`** to split Martian `CO₂` into **`122 grams` of `98%`-pure breathable oxygen** (`FACT: Made 122g breathable oxygen`).
