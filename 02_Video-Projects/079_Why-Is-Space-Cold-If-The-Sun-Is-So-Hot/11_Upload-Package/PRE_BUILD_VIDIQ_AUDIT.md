**Chapter list after audit** (5 chapters told as cause and effect, each with a teach-point + `Teach on screen` markers):

1. **A `5,500 °C` Furnace in an Empty Room: Heat vs. Temperature in a Vacuum** — Why the Sun's core burns at **`15 million °C`** and its photosphere radiates **`3.828 × 10²⁶ watts`** at **`5,500 °C` (`5,772 K`)**, yet temperature measures the jostling speed of atoms (`DATA` #1 + `SCALE: 2.5 × 10¹⁹ air molecules per cm³ at sea level on Earth vs ~5 particles per cm³ in interplanetary space at 1 AU`). Because vacuum has almost no atoms to conduct or convect heat, **`1,361 W/m²`** of sunlight travels `149.6 million km` to Earth without heating the empty space in between (`FACT: 5 particles per cubic cm`).
2. **Boiling and Freezing at the Same Time: `+121 °C` and `-157 °C` in Earth Orbit** — What happens when those `1,361 W/m²` of solar photons hit a solid object in low Earth orbit (`WHEN: 1965–Present`): with no air to carry heat from the lit side to the dark side, direct sunlight bakes the front of an astronaut's white spacesuit to **`+121 °C` (`+250 °F`)** while the shadowed back plunges to **`-157 °C` (`-250 °F`)** — a **`278 °C` (`500 °F`) split across one suit** (`DATA` #2 + `SCALE: +121 °C in orbital sunlight vs -157 °C in shadow = 278 °C difference across a single spacesuit`). How NASA's `14-layer` **Extravehicular Mobility Unit (`EMU`)** reflects `85%` of sunlight and circulates chilled water through **`91.4 metres` (`300 feet`)** of `LCVG` tubing (`FACT: 91 metres of cooling tubes`).
3. **The `318 °C` Tennis Court: How the James Webb Space Telescope Stays at `-233 °C`** — Midpoint engineering twist ("Case closed? Wrong — in space, sunlight is so intense that an infrared telescope will blind itself unless you block every watt"). How NASA Nobel laureate **John Mather** (`WHO: John Mather (born 1946) — JWST senior project scientist`) and engineers placed **`JWST`** `1.5 million km` away at `L₂` (`WHEN: December 2021 – January 2022`) behind a **5-layer, `21.2 × 14.2 m` Kapton sunshield** that drops temperatures by **`318 °C`** across two metres: from **`+85 °C` (`358 K`)** on sun-facing Layer 1 (`up to a 383 K / +110 °C design maximum, ~370 K measured in flight`) to **`-233 °C` (`40 K`)** on the mirror side (`36 K / -237 °C design minimum`), while a helium cryocooler chills `MIRI` to **`6.7 Kelvin` (`-266.5 °C`)** (`DATA` #3 + `SCALE: +85 °C on JWST Sunshield Layer 1 vs -233 °C on Layer 5 and 6.7 K inside MIRI`).
4. **Why Deep Space Never Hits Absolute Zero: The `2.725-Kelvin` Afterglow of the Big Bang (`1964–1965`)** — What happens if you leave every star and galaxy behind? In **1964–1965** (`WHEN: 1964–1965`), Bell Labs radio astronomers **Arno Penzias** (`WHO: Arno Penzias (1933–2024) — Cosmic background co-discoverer`) and **Robert Wilson** (`WHO: Robert Wilson (born 1936) — Cosmic background co-discoverer`), alongside Princeton physicist **Robert Dicke** (`WHO: Robert Dicke (1916–1997) — Princeton CMB physicist`), discovered the **Cosmic Microwave Background (`CMB`)** — `~411` relic photons per cubic centimetre from **`380,000 years` after the Big Bang** that keep even the emptiest intergalactic void at **`2.725 Kelvin` (`-270.425 °C`)**, matched to a blackbody curve within `0.005%` (`50 ppm`) of peak brightness by `COBE FIRAS` (`WHEN: 1990–1994`) and measured to `0.02%` temperature uncertainty (`2.72548 ± 0.00057 K`, `Fixsen 2009`).
5. **Colder Than the Big Bang: Inside the `1-Kelvin` Boomerang Nebula (`1995–2013`)** — How can anything in nature get colder than the `2.725 K` background of the universe? In **1995** (published **1997**, `WHEN: 1995–1997`), astronomers **Raghvendra Sahai** (`WHO: Raghvendra Sahai (born 1956) — Boomerang Nebula co-discoverer`) and **Lars-Åke Nyman** (`WHO: Lars-Åke Nyman (born 1953) — SEST telescope astronomer`) pointed the `15-metre` `SEST` telescope in Chile at the **Boomerang Nebula** `5,000 light-years` away (`confirmed by ALMA in 2013`, `WHEN: 2013`) and found a dying red giant blasting gas outward at **`164 km/s` (`590,000 km/h`)** — expanding so fast that adiabatic cooling drops the nebula to **`1 Kelvin` (`-272.15 °C`)**, colder than deep space itself (`FACT: 1 Kelvin, colder than space`). *(Note for `SOURCES.md`: every living scientist's `WHO:` birth year in Chapters 3–5 — John Mather, Robert Wilson, Raghvendra Sahai, and Lars-Åke Nyman — must carry an exact NASA/Nobel/institutional biographical quote in `SOURCES.md` before script lock.)*
