2. **Inside the Roche Limit: Torn Into `21` Pieces (`7 July 1992`)** — Tracing its orbit backward, **Brian Marsden** (`WHO: Brian Marsden (1937–2010) — Minor Planet Center director`) and **Paul Chodas** (`WHO: Paul Chodas (born 1952) — NASA JPL orbital dynamicist`) discovered that on **7 July 1992** (`WHEN: 7 July 1992`) the comet had skimmed just **`~40,000 km` above Jupiter's cloud tops (`1.36` Jovian radii)** — deep inside Jupiter's **Roche limit**. As **Erik Asphaug** (`WHO: Erik Asphaug (born 1961) — Planetary impact physicist`, `Asphaug & Benz 1994`) showed in *Nature*, differential tidal gravity tore the **`~1.8-km` rubble-pile nucleus** into **21 fragments (`A` through `W`)** stretched across `1.1 million km` (`DATA` #1 + `SCALE: 1.1-million-km fragment train = nearly 3x the 384,400-km Earth-Moon distance`) — and aimed all `21` straight at Jupiter for **July 1994**.
