Gliese 710 is an orange dwarf star heading towards the Solar System. In about 1.3 million years, its predicted journey through the distant Oort Cloud could reshape the comet traffic around the Sun — while staying far beyond the planets.

Follow the Gaia measurements, the scale of the flyby and the changing night sky, from a star we cannot see unaided today to a future bright orange visitor.
