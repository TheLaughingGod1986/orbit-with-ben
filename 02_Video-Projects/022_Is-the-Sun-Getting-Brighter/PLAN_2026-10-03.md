### Script v02 fixes (Claude)

1. "across a million years … a little more severe" → **a hundred million years … about one percent more severe** (a million years is 0.01%, not visible).
2. "Ten percent takes about **eleven** of those steps" → **ten** (10 × 1% ≈ 10.5% compounded, about 1.1 billion years).
3. "Would you survive it?" → "Could a shoreline survive it?" (the film is about oceans, not the viewer).
4. The vague end line becomes the **Venus handoff**: "Next door, there is a planet that may already have taken that path. Next week: what happened to Venus."

The numbers are locked to `SOURCES.md`: 1% per 110 million years and a moist-greenhouse limit near 1 billion years (Schröder & Connon Smith 2008); an early Sun near 70% of today's light; a sunspot-cycle wobble of about 0.1%.
