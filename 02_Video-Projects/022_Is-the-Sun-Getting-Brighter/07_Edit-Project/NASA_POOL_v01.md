# Sun (022): verified NASA picture and motion pool v01

Built 1 Oct 2026 for *Is the Sun Getting Brighter?* (script `01_Script/sun_brighter_script_master_v01.md`).
There are 68 NASA stills and 11 NASA Goddard (SVS) motion clips. Every still was checked by eye on contact sheets: no labels, inset boxes, watermarks, coronagraph timestamps, or hardware in frame. Every ID resolves on images-api.nasa.gov. The same rules as the Saturn pool apply (`docs/ORBIT_PLAYBOOK_LESSONS.md`: framing, row times from VO word timestamps, credits from the pool).

## What the script asks for, and what covers it

| Script beat | Covered by |
|---|---|
| Frame 0: prominence mid-rise, Sun filling the frame | **Real SDO motion** (SVS 10925, 11517, 5649), not AI |
| 'The wrong clock': prominences, the 11-year wobble | S1 and S2 stills; SVS 3548/3549 (min vs max), 11072, 10804 |
| 'A slow upward line beside an eleven-year wobble' | **Code graphic** (to build): the three clocks, drawn from the script's numbers |
| The core diagram: hydrogen to helium, the core tightening | **Code graphic** (to build). Background: SVS 31400 (simulated interior flows) |
| Young dim Sun over an early shore + Orbit | Approved AI route only (Omni for Orbit). NASA has nothing literal. S4 has one early-Earth illustration and SVS 11853 |
| A brighter Sun over a thinning ocean haze + Orbit | S5 limb and ocean stills; Orbit via Omni |
| End: return to the opening Sun in motion | SDO motion (a different stretch from the open) |

Every still carries a small instrument timestamp in a bottom corner (SDO frames often do). Crop it with the framing push. **Never show a coronagraph frame** with its date stamp (these were left out on purpose).

## Science guard rails for picture choice

- **Prominences, flares and CMEs are 'the wrong clock'.** Captions never imply they are the brightening.
- **Sunspot pictures illustrate the ~0.1% wobble,** never the 1% per 110 Myr climb.
- **No red giant anywhere.** The script stops before it ('belongs to another film').
- **Wavelength-coloured SDO frames (gold, teal, red) are false colour.** Fine as 'how instruments see the Sun', but the 'light on your face' lines want a white-light disc (S2 PIA19876, PIA21783, GSFC e000923) or a sunrise from orbit (S6 iss074e0494675).
- **Earth pictures show today's Earth.** The 'thinning ocean haze' future is drawn by framing and the narration, never by a doctored NASA photo.
