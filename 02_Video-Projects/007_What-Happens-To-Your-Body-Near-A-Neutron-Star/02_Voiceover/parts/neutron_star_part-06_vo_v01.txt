Falling in a uniform field can feel like floating. That’s the cruelty.

Near a neutron star, the field is not uniform over your height. The pull on your nearer side outruns the pull on your farther side. That difference — the tide — is what turns “approach” into dissection.

Would you feel yourself being crushed — or would it be too fast?

Honestly? For many trajectories, the catastrophic part arrives so violently that “pain” is the wrong story. Structures fail. Chemistry fails. The narrative of a continuous self ends while the physics is still accelerating.

Could you survive one second?

It depends where that second is spent.
