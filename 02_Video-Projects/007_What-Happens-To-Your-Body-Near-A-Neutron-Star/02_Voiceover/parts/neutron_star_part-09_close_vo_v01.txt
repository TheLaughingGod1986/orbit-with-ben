You would not stand, touch, or casually explore a neutron star. You would meet a leftover engine of stellar death where light bends, energy shifts, and the body stops being a useful unit of storytelling.

If this is what matter becomes when a star loses… what does that say about the limits of everything we call solid — including the ground under your feet tonight?

Orbit turns away, thrusters finally winning a little distance. The star shrinks from apocalypse back into a fierce jewel.

Behind them, the galaxy looks almost normal again.

Almost.

Because once you’ve seen light lean… you never quite trust a straight line.
