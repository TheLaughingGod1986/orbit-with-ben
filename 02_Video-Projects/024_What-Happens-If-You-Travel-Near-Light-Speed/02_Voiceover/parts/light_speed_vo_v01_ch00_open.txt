You leave Earth at ninety-nine percent of the speed of light. Why does home come back older than you?

You are gone for one year by your own watch. You land, and seven years have passed at home. Nobody cheated. Your watch is fine. Time itself ran at a different pace for you. The danger was never the speed. It was your own clock.

That is not science fiction. Your phone already depends on it every day. In this film I will show you why a moving clock runs slow, what a trip near light speed would do to you and the people you leave behind, and why you can never quite reach the limit.
