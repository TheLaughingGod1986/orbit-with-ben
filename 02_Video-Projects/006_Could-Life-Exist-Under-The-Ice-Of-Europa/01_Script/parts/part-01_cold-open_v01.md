## Narration (Part 01 only)

Jupiter fills the sky.

Not a postcard. A giant. Storm bands rolling like weather on a world that could swallow a thousand Earths.

And beside it — small, bright, cracked white — Europa.

Orbit drifts closer. Cream eyes wide. The ice is not smooth. It is scarred — a frozen ocean shell, cut with long dark lines like something moved underneath… and left fingerprints in the cold.

What if the most important ocean in our solar system… is the one we cannot see?

Not on the surface. Under the ice. Dark. Pressed. Liquid.

Orbit tips toward the cracks. Antenna glowing. The journey is not past this moon. The journey is *through* it — into whatever waits below the frozen lid.

Could life exist under the ice of Europa?
