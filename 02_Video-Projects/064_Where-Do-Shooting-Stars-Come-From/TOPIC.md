### Story beats (all numbers to verify)

1. What a shooting star is: dust hitting the air at tens of kilometres a second, burning roughly 70-100 km up.
2. Where the dust comes from: comets shed it along their orbits; Earth crosses the trails on the same dates each year.
3. Halley's Comet: two showers from one comet (Eta Aquariids, Orionids); the comet itself is next due in 2061.
4. The Perseids and comet Swift-Tuttle; the Geminids, which come from an asteroid (3200 Phaethon).
5. When dust is bigger: fireballs, and the pieces that reach the ground.
