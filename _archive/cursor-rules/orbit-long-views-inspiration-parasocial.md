## Parasocial (source of truth)

https://youtu.be/Xf_J7kBzxvo — “How to Trick YouTube into Pushing Your Channel” by Marcus Jones (@Marcus-Jones), 23 Jun 2026, with Mario Joos (retention strategist).

**Argument:** MrBeast swap — algorithm = audience. Views compound when a first-time viewer comes back. That return is a one-sided parasocial bond.

**Attraction first:** social likability + task competence beat looks. Physical attraction is the weak link.

Then **all four** speech components must be present or the moment does not count:

1. **Intimacy** — talk TO one viewer (coffee-shop rule), not AT an audience. Eye-line at the lens. Early MrBeast vs later announcer.
2. **Continuity** — repeat that connection many times in the same film (ten “you” beats, not one intro hook).
3. **Simulated reciprocity** — sound like you care / noticed them: “you’re probably thinking…”, name a real comment, pin a good one, 3s of something you’d only show a friend (Orbit on the bench, Ben’s observing night).
4. **Persona** — same Orbit + Ben next Thursday. Don’t reset the tone.

**First minute:** one “save the cat” beat that makes a stranger like Orbit / Ben before the lecture. If not funny, lean on task attraction (you clearly know the physics) and let the edit carry a joke.

**Close** is a **next-film** Studio end screen — not a subscribe lecture, not a shop.
