# Orbit — YouTube growth and policy (locked 25 Sep 2026)

The algorithm follows the audience. A film grows when a stranger stays and comes back.

## Get more views

- **The first moments:** the picture is already moving. A long has a strange picture in the first 3 seconds, with no logo and no slow intro card. A Short has that picture in the first second.
- **Thumbnail and title do different jobs.** The thumbnail grabs attention. The title creates curiosity. They are not the same words, and both deliver the thing the film actually shows.
- **Click-through rate.** Treat a live film under about 4% CTR as a thumb or title that needs a change. Prefer the 4–7% band. A higher score that breaks the promise, or that uses fear, does not ship.
- **Holding the watch.** Edit out pauses, frozen stills, and fluff. Keep average percentage viewed above about 50% on a film that is doing its job. YouTube promotes a film that keeps people watching.

## Keep them watching

- **The first 30 seconds.** Start on the picture the title and thumbnail promised. Sentence one is that promise. No spinning logo, no theme-song open, no “who I am” intro. Hint at the payoff that comes later.
- **The picture keeps changing.** About every 4–6 seconds, change what is on screen: a new view, on-screen words for the thing just named, or a quiet sound cue when a graphic appears. A frozen frame is a drop. A Short stays 22–27 seconds.
- **The story moves.** Connect beats with cause and effect: this happened, but that failed, therefore the next thing. A flat list of facts loses people.
- **No goodbye.** Do not say “in conclusion,” “to wrap up,” “thanks for watching,” or “that’s all.” Finish the last point at a normal pace and hand them to the next film with the Studio end screen.

## Stay in good standing

- **Copyright.** Do not use music, movie clips, or game footage we do not have the rights to. Orbit’s own score and voice are the default.
- **Honest metadata.** Titles and tags match the film. A title that makes people click and leave is a failed title.
- **Advertiser-friendly.** No heavy swearing in the first 30 seconds, no extreme violence, no controversial medical advice. Wonder, not dread.

## Consistency

- One long a week and three Shorts, on the locked days. A realistic week that ships beats three rushed films.
- Quality over extra uploads. A held, honest film beats a padded one.
