# Social mirrors — one unique post (Ben 25 Aug 2026)

Each YouTube Short may appear **once** on Instagram, **once** on Facebook, and **once** on Threads. That is the post itself — not two Reels of the same clip, not a remake of a title already mirrored.

In any stretch of ~20–30 social posts, every item must be a **different** video (title / file slug). A new YouTube id of the same Short does not unlock a second post.

TikTok stays fully paused (`orbit-tiktok-upload-ban.mdc`).
