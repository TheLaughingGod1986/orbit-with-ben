World War 2 documentaries and military history — naval convoys, air power, wartime engineering, and the logistics that decided WW2.

Iron & Compass explores how the Second World War was engineered, navigated, supplied, and fought across every theatre — from North Atlantic wolfpack battles and Pacific floating dry docks to high-altitude bomber programmes and amphibious invasions.

Every documentary combines verified US and UK public-domain archive photography, custom-drawn operational maps, and primary-source military histories and technical records.

New long-form WW2 documentaries every Tuesday and Saturday at 17:00 London (12:00 ET).
