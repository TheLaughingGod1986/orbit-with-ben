# `WW2:005` shot list (J3001)

The rule (RULES.md §5): US federal public domain or UK Crown copyright expired only. No agency or wire photos, no private donations, no AI video. Maps and diagrams are drawn in code (licence: own).

**Collection IDs are not filled in yet, on purpose.** This job had no live catalogue access, so the archive column gives the collection and a search phrase, not a made-up number. Before assembly, the picture job looks up each item, records the real ID, and checks the licence line on the catalogue page, all in `03_Picture/ARCHIVE_PROVENANCE.md`. An item that turns out not to be Crown or US federal is replaced with its fallback.

**Excluded:** German photographs of Campbeltown wedged on the caisson (Bundesarchiv CC BY-SA; IWM HU series), because they are not Crown or US federal. See `TOPIC_REVIEW.md`.
