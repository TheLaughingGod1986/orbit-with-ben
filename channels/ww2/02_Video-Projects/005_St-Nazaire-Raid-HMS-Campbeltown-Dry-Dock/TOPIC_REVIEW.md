## The limit, which shapes the shot list

The best-known photographs of Campbeltown wedged on the caisson were taken by **German** photographers on the morning of 28 March 1942. They are held as Bundesarchiv items (CC BY-SA) and in IWM's HU series (captured or private material, **not** Crown copyright). RULES.md §5 allows only US federal public domain and Crown copyright expired, so **they are excluded**. The wedged ship is shown with a code-drawn diagram (G7) and with RAF reconnaissance photographs taken after the raid. The thumbnail uses Campbeltown at sea in British service, or USS Buchanan from NHHC, not the wedged image.
