## Original Angle

After the August 1942 Dieppe raid proved seizing a defended French port intact was suicidal, British and US engineers secretly built two Dover-sized harbours (Mulberry A and Mulberry B) in pieces around Britain — 212 6,000-ton concrete Phoenix caissons, flexible steel Whale roadways rising 24 feet with the Normandy tide, Bombardons, Gooseberry blockships, and PLUTO undersea fuel pipelines — towed 1.5 million tons across the Channel at 4 knots, and survived the 19–22 June 1944 gale.
