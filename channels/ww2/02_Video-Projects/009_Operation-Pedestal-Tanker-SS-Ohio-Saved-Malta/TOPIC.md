## Original Angle

With Malta weeks from starvation and out of Spitfire aviation fuel in August 1942, Operation Pedestal sent 14 fast merchantmen and 4 carriers through a four-day Axis air, E-boat, and submarine gauntlet. Nine merchantmen and HMS Eagle were sunk; the torpedoed, bombed tanker SS Ohio (11,500 tons of fuel) entered Valletta's Grand Harbour held afloat only by two Royal Navy destroyers lashed to her sides.
