## Six-Chapter Outline (`25:00`)

1. **`0:00–3:30` Cold Open — March 1943: Four Days From Severing Britain's Lifeline:** `Convoys HX 229` & `SC 122` (`22` merchantmen, `146,000 tons` sunk for `1` U-boat lost); the Admiralty's secret assessment that the Allies came within days of abandoning the North Atlantic convoy system.
2. **`3:30–8:00` Chapter 1 — Geometry of the "Black Pit":** Why air bases in Northern Ireland, Iceland, and Newfoundland left a `600-mile` unpatrolled oval south of Greenland where wolfpacks (`40+` Type VIIc boats) recharged on the surface in broad daylight and outran `16-knot` escorts.
3. **`8:00–13:00` Chapter 2 — Fewer Than Forty VLR Liberators:** Why strategic bombing commands held onto long-range airframes while stripping belly turrets and adding bomb-bay tanks to just a few dozen `B-24 Liberators` (`No. 120 Squadron RAF`, `2,400-mile` range) could reach the centre of the Pit (`WHO: Max Horton`, `WHO: John Slessor`).
4. **`13:00–17:30` Chapter 3 — Invisible Beams (`10 cm` Cavity Magnetron Radar, Leigh Light & `HF/DF`):** How `ASV Mk III` / `Type 271` centimetric radar defeated German `Metox` receivers in fog, while shipboard `HF/DF` ("Huff-Duff") fixed U-boat wolfpack radio transmissions in seconds.
5. **`17:30–21:30` Chapter 4 — Midpoint Climax: Convoy `ONS 5` (`29 April – 6 May 1943`):** Storm-scattered merchantmen off Greenland meet `43` U-boats (`Group Fink`) — and radar-guided escorts (`HMS Duncan`, `HMS Vidette`, `Hedgehog` forward-throwing mortars) plus VLR Liberators destroy **7 U-boats** in zero-visibility fog.
6. **`21:30–25:00` Chapter 5 — "Black May" & Dönitz's Withdrawal (`24 May 1943`):** Escort carriers (`USS Bogue`, `HMS Biter`) and Support Groups close the gap permanently; `43` U-boats lost in May alone (`25%` of operational crews); Dönitz pulls wolfpacks out of the North Atlantic on `24 May 1943`.
