The Allies closed the U-boats’ Atlantic refuge through longer-range aircraft, escort carriers, better detection and the ships protecting the convoys.

Why did air cover matter, and why was a long-range bomber only part of the answer? This film follows the problem from the March convoy battles through May 1943.
