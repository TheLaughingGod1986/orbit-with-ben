# Picture plan — WW2:002

Every spoken sentence has a row in SHOT_LIST_v03.csv. Each row names its archive original or a uniquely identified code map/schematic to build for that sentence. This is a production shot list, not rendered picture. Actual duration/cuts follow the recorded words; target roughly 6–9 seconds per visual state, divide a long sentence into successive labelled states without stretching VO.

The inherited 65% archive / 35% maps was aspirational and is superseded here by an evidence-led map-heavy treatment: five catalogue originals, deliberate returns to two photographs as evidence, and bespoke map states. Do not loop these five pictures as generic filler. A future archive enrichment may substitute only an exact rights-checked image supporting the same line. The complete map alternatives mean no unspecified asset is needed to understand the film.

Normal/bright monochrome archive grade, restrained ink/naval-chart colours, 16:9. No OWB/HOS templates, robot, Explorer or borrowed footage. Natural Earth coastlines: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ (public domain); no copied Google maps. Label all battle diagrams SCHEMATIC; no precise battle track or invented range circle. Maps are an explanatory plan, not a claim we have rendered or visually reviewed them.

Opening: immediately show convoy continuing beyond shore patrol reach, escorts still present. First paragraph is about 24 seconds at the planned pace. Chapter changes are editorial estimates in CHAPTERS_v03.json.
