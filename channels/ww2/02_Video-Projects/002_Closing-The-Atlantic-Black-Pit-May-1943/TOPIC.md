## Original Angle

In March 1943 (Convoys HX 229 & SC 122), U-boats sank 627,000 tons of shipping by hunting inside the 600-mile Mid-Atlantic Air Gap ('the Black Pit') south of Greenland. Eight weeks later ('Black May' 1943), Dönitz lost 43 U-boats and withdrew after fewer than 40 modified 2,400-mile VLR B-24 Liberators, escort carriers, 10 cm centimetric radar, and shipboard HF/DF radio triangulation converged.
