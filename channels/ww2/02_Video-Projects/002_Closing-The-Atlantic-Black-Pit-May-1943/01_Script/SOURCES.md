## Corrections to the inherited outline

- Remove fixed 600-mile width and 800/1,100-mile coverage circles: no single evidenced operational boundary. Maps show qualitative reach only.
- Remove 627,000 tons supposedly sunk in the two March convoys: conflates monthly totals with a specific action; no aggregate used.
- Remove “four days from severing Britain's lifeline” and imminent abandonment of convoys: not established by evidence read.
- Remove fewer-than-40 fleet total, 2,400-mile range and 25% of operational crews: definitions/configurations/denominators not established.
- Do not mix Type 271 ship radar and ASV Mk III aircraft radar as one device; explain distinct sensor roles.
- Remove “seven U-boats destroyed by VLR Liberators in fog” at ONS 5. RCAF p.554 directly contradicts that mechanism; surface radar matters.
- Do not treat HF/DF as a decoded message or an exact single-bearing fix.
- Do not claim all VLR aircraft had Leigh Lights; explain the radar/searchlight combination in its documented role.
- Do not use the 43-boat monthly total without its scope; no numerical loss chart is commissioned.
- Do not call the gap permanently and perfectly watched, or say the war ended in May.
- Keep the title's “safest” as relative protection from land-based aircraft, explained in the opening; it is not a statistical ranking of all submarine theatres.

## Supplemental direct checks after the partial independent audit

R9–R12 were opened directly on 18 November, not inferred from search titles. R1 explicitly names Admiral Sir Max Horton in the opening command-structure discussion, and later identifies him with Western Approaches; it dates Slessor’s tenure as three months by May 1943. R4 printed p.553 explicitly calls ONS 5 westbound. R12 dates its attack table 28/29/30 followed by 4/5/6 May. R10 pp.101–103 describes the searchlight and its night attack use. R11 confirms cargo types; R1 p.38 independently supports troops and equipment for Bolero. R4 p.555 dates an earlier call-off to 21 May; the spoken 24 May general withdrawal follows the explicit dated Royal Navy anniversary account R9, corroborated by R5. This difference is preserved rather than hidden.
