## Conflicts and limits resolved before drafting

- S1 p.105 gives **24 July 1943** for Nimitz’s order; S2 gives **24 June 1943**. Narration uses **summer 1943**. The exact order needs an original dated order before either precise date appears on screen.
- S1 p.109 says “By January 1944 torpedo performance was almost uniformly good”; S3 gives autumn 1943 for satisfactory operation. These are retained as attributed milestones, not conflated into a universal 21-month repair deadline.
- S1 p.108 reports **15 fired / 12 known hits claimed**, not 15 hits. Counts are explicitly attributed, with no shot-by-shot animation pretending to reconstruct the attack.
- S1 pp.97–98 gives multiple depth causes. Do not say heavier heads alone explain the full error.
- S1 p.105 contradicts the original brief’s pairing of Christie with Lockwood as fellow opponents of the magnetic mechanism. The narration corrects it.
- S1 p.101 concerns **destructive** magnetic tests. The script never says there were no tests at all.
- S1 is institutional history, not an original Tinosa patrol report. Its preface admits bias. No purported exact Daspit quotation, attack position, tanker tonnage, aircraft-propeller anecdote or universal failure percentage is included.
- Conversion in P16: 10 international feet ×0.3048 =3.048 metres, rounded to “about three metres”. This is calculation, not a new historical measurement.
