## Original Angle

The Manhattan Project cost $1.9 billion; designing, tooling, and fielding the Boeing B-29 Superfortress cost $3.0 billion — the costliest weapons programme of WW2. Ordered off the drawing board before a prototype flew, it combined pressurised crew tunnels, GE analog-computer gun turrets, and magnesium-crankcase Wright R-3350 engines whose fires killed test pilot Eddie Allen, triggered the freezing 'Battle of Kansas', and hit a 200-mph jet stream over Tokyo.
