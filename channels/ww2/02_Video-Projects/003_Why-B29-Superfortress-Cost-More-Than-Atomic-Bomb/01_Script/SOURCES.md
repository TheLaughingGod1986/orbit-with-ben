# WW2:003 — Sources

One line per claim tag `[C##]` in `SCRIPT.md`. Sources are named by work and chapter or section; page numbers are to be added at the claims check where the copy is to hand. "Check" marks a claim the writer is less sure of, for `CLAIMS_CHECK.md` to settle before VO.

## Works cited

- **[CC5]** Craven, W. F. & Cate, J. L. (eds.), *The Army Air Forces in World War II*, Vol. V: *The Pacific: Matterhorn to Nagasaki* (Univ. of Chicago Press for the Office of Air Force History, 1953). US government history.
- **[CC6]** Craven & Cate (eds.), Vol. VI: *Men and Planes* (1955), chapters on the VLR bomber programme and B-29 production.
- **[KN]** Knaack, M. S., *Encyclopedia of U.S. Air Force Aircraft and Missile Systems*, Vol. II: *Post-World War II Bombers 1945–1973* (Office of Air Force History, 1988), B-29 and B-50 entries.
- **[USSBS]** United States Strategic Bombing Survey, *Summary Report (Pacific War)* (1946); and *The Effects of Air Attack on Japanese Urban Economy* / *Effects of Incendiary Bomb Attacks on Japan* (1947).
- **[NMUSAF]** National Museum of the U.S. Air Force, fact sheets "Boeing B-29 Superfortress", "Boeing XB-29", "Consolidated B-32 Dominator", "Battle of Kansas".
- **[VM]** Vander Meulen, J., *Building the B-29* (Smithsonian Institution Press, 1995).
- **[HL]** HistoryLink.org, essay on the crash of the Boeing XB-29 into the Frye packing plant, Seattle, 18 February 1943.
- **[AA]** Schwartz, S. I. (ed.), *Atomic Audit: The Costs and Consequences of U.S. Nuclear Weapons Since 1940* (Brookings, 1998), Manhattan Project cost through 1945.
- **[HA]** Hewlett, R. G. & Anderson, O. E., *The New World, 1939–1946* (US Atomic Energy Commission history, 1962).
- **[LM]** LeMay, C. E. with Kantor, M., *Mission with LeMay* (Doubleday, 1965).
- **[FB]** Frank, R. B., *Downfall: The End of the Imperial Japanese Empire* (Random House, 1999), on the March 1945 change of tactics and casualty estimates.
- **[TR]** US Senate, Special Committee Investigating the National Defense Program (Truman Committee), report on the Wright Aeronautical Corporation, 1943.
- **[CAF]** Commemorative Air Force (FIFI) and Doc's Friends (Doc), operators of the two airworthy B-29s.
