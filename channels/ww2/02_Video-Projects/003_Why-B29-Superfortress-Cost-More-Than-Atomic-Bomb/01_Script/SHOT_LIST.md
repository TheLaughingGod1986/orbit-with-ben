# WW2:003 — Shot list (draft 1, J2997)

One row per script paragraph `[S##]`. **Archive IDs are not filled in yet, on purpose:** no catalogue ID is written here until someone has opened that record and checked its licence. The picture job (J2998) finds each image, writes its collection ID and licence into the empty columns, and logs it in the film's licence log. Every image must be US federal public domain (NARA, LoC FSA/OWI, USAF/NMUSAF, US Navy NHHC) or Crown copyright expired (IWM, with an expired Crown notice). Maps and graphics are drawn in code (G#). No Veo planned. No realistic depiction of real people or events. Nothing graphic: the March 1945 raid (S55–S56) uses aerial reconnaissance stills and a map, not ground photographs of victims.

Code graphics: **G1** cost bars ($1.9bn vs $3.0bn) · **G2** range map: 1940 bases vs 5,000 mi requirement · **G3** cabin cross-section: nose, tunnel, waist, tail · **G4** GE fire-control sight-line diagram · **G5** order timeline (May 1941 250 → Sep 1942 >1,600, first flight marker) · **G6** four-plant map (Wichita, Renton, Marietta, Omaha) · **G7** R-3350 two-row cooling diagram · **G8** Matterhorn map (India → Hump → Chengdu → Yawata) with fuel-trip counter · **G9** Marianas map (Saipan/Tinian/Guam → Tokyo ~1,500 mi; Iwo Jima) · **G10** jet-stream drift diagram at 30,000 ft · **G11** altitude profile, high day vs low night · **G12** legacy line (B-29 → B-50, C-97/KC-97, Stratocruiser).
