An Asteroid Will Skim Past Earth on Friday the 13th

Apophis, 340 metres wide, passes 32,000 kilometres up on 13 April 2029, inside the ring of satellites at about 36,000 kilometres. It will miss.

Sources: NASA, Apophis Facts (340 m wide; 32,000 km from the surface on 13 April 2029; geosynchronous satellites about 36,000 km up; no impact risk for at least 100 years).
https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/asteroids/apophis-facts/
