## 3. Conflicts for a Chief decision before any change

1. **Rule 24 against the deferred number.** Rule 24 wants the subject and the surprise spoken by 1.5 s; the ruling wants the main count or scale number kept out of sentence one. When the number is the surprise (hook text such as `15 METERS / DEEP`, `ONLY 10 M / THICK`), both cannot hold. Options: (a) sentence one says the surprise in words without the figure, and the figure comes in sentence two or later, with the hook caption also not showing the figure; (b) the figure may stay in the frame-0 hook caption but is not spoken until after sentence one; (c) rule 24 wins and the deferral applies only where the number is not the surprise. Recommended: (c) until the readout is in, because it changes no standing rule. None of the three Shorts needs this decided to air.
2. **Keeper line 1.** The Keeper format needs one question in the title and in line 1. That fits the ruling (a question holds the number back), so no conflict, but a Keeper Short can never test "number in sentence one" the other way.
3. **Locked voice on lifted Shorts.** Every Short cut from a long (J0979) uses the long's locked words. The count/scale change can only be met by choosing which sentences to lift, never by editing them.
4. **032 first 30 seconds and Huygens:** see section 4. The locked voice does not name Huygens in the first 30 seconds.
