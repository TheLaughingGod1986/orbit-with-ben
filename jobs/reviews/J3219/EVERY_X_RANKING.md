# J3219 step 1: every-X topics ranked (provisional)

Written 20 Nov 2026 by `macbook-2`. Spend: £0, 0 vidIQ credits.

**What this is and is not.** The brief asks for a ranking by search demand and competition from vidIQ keyword research (at most 15 credits). vidIQ is not usable from this MacBook lane (the tool is refused), so no monthly search volumes or competition scores were read. This ranking uses only what is already saved in each topic's folder: the signed-out YouTube search pulls (`11_Upload-Package/evidence_*_public_search.json`), the neighbour pulls and each `TOPIC_OPPORTUNITY_SCORE.md`. It is a ranking by **what the same kind of video already earns for other channels**, which is a stand-in for demand, not a measurement of it. The vidIQ pass is still owed before a script is written (see "Left to do").

## How it was ranked

1. **Same-format proof (main measure).** The most views on a video with the same shape and subject ("Every X Explained", a complete tour, about 5 to 20 minutes), from the saved search pull. This is the nearest evidence to "people look for this and click it".
2. **Can a small channel win it.** Whether the same-format videos that did well are from small or mid channels, and what the weakest same-format uploads got (the floor).
3. **Our own numbers.** Whether our Shorts or longs on the subject did well.
4. **Risk.** Scope that cannot be toured honestly in ten minutes, overlap with a film we have, or a topic review that is not a GO.

The opportunity scores do not separate the topics (seven are 95, two are 96), so they were not used to order them.
