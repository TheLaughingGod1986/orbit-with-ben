## Why Voyager Plan 8B fails

1. **Earliest answer, not the chosen marker.** Frame zero says `ONE LIGHT-DAY / AWAY`. The next spoken sentence says that a message travelling at light speed takes a whole day. With its source interval 9.980–13.720 mapped to output 2.400–6.140, “whole” lands at `2.400 + 12.460 - 9.980 = 4.880 s` (26.7% of 18.300 s), and “day” at 5.200 s (28.4%). This answers the distance question. Calling only the later repeated “one light day” the payoff does not satisfy the rule. That repeated answer at 12.840 s also falls inside the 9–14 s title banner, despite the preparation's summary claiming it is clear of the banner. The proposed `24 HOURS` visual would disclose it early too.
2. **Wrong assets, confirmed by viewing the actual NAS files.** `PIA23645.jpg` is the Pale Blue Dot image, not a Deep Space Network radio-link illustration. `PIA21741.jpg` shows preparation of the Golden Record, not a heliosphere trajectory view. `PIA17464.jpg` shows a launch, not Voyager in interstellar space. The existing pool agrees with these observations; dimensions and hashes are in the evidence JSON. Hash presence alone cannot validate a scene description.
3. **Motion is not measured yet.** A 1.00→1.03 spacecraft push with a cycling background does not establish a moving payoff subject or the still-only exception (at least 15% scale change / clear pan inside one second AND a visible event by 0.5 s). The 6.2–10.1 s shot is explicitly another still pan/push, overlapping the 4–8 s no-static-zoom window. Predicted `f0diff >= 2.20`, `motion >= 12`, `scdet > 15` and loop difference are not gate outputs. Cycling a 66-frame background within a 72-frame open also needs inspection for replay; no approval is inferred from Neptune's different render.
4. **Not a one-field repair.** Removing the caption alone leaves the early spoken answer. Moving that sentence alone still leaves repeated distance answers, the banner collision, the wrong plates and unresolved motion. I will not dispatch it as a locked, feasible plan on that basis.
